# Specialty review: cardiology (2026-10-23)

Scope. Cardiology view of `merged-conclusion.md` (bottom lines cover fasting insulin, HOMA-IR, TyG and TG/HDL-C; post-load and C-peptide were not weighed as predictors). Cardiology rows of `usecases-verified.md` (rows 13, 17) and `reviews/scope-audit.md` were read. Skeptic files `reviews/skeptic-merged.md` and `reviews/skeptic-steelman.md` were read for what is already corrected. Analysis reports and other specialty files were not read. No deliverable, score file or claims file was edited.

Assumptions. The task prompt set a limit of 25 PubMed or web look-ups. I used 24 (log in section 6). Pages were read through the web-fetch tool, which returns query-focused excerpts of the full page, not the whole document. Every "read level" below is therefore "excerpts of full text" at best, never "read end to end". A "not found" statement from me means "not found in the excerpts retrieved". Measure type is stated for every claim: F = fasting insulin or HOMA-IR; T = TyG or TG/HDL-C; P = post-load; C = C-peptide; E = eGDR (clinical-variable surrogate, no assay).

## 2. Missed evidence

A. 2026 ACC/AHA multisociety Dyslipidemia Guideline (DOI 10.1161/CIR.0000000000001423). Excerpts show: PREVENT-based risk estimation; for adults aged 40 to 75 without ASCVD or diabetes and with persistent triglycerides 150 to 499 mg/dL, estimate 10-year ASCVD risk with PREVENT to guide a benefit-risk discussion (COR 1); risk enhancers include CKM syndrome and persistent triglycerides but not insulin, HOMA-IR or TyG; the CKM definition lists "hyperinsulinemia" among common characteristics; the lifestyle text advises "improvement of insulin resistance" without any measurement; the reference list for the imaging section cites a 2025 paper on TyG and vulnerable plaques in T2D (reference only; the sentence it supports was not seen). Read level: excerpts. A whole-document read would be needed before writing "no recommendation".

B. PREVENT as the benchmark (PMID 37947085). Base predictors: smoking, systolic BP, cholesterol, antihypertensive and statin use, diabetes, eGFR. No insulin and no triglycerides. Optional: urine albumin-creatinine ratio, HbA1c, social deprivation index. Measure type: none of F, T, P, C, E. Use: sets the expected size of an add-on (delta C about 0.004 to 0.005 for three optional markers together).

C. AHA CKM Presidential Advisory (DOI 10.1161/CIR.0000000000001184). Excerpts: staging uses hypertriglyceridaemia (135 mg/dL or more), hypertension, metabolic syndrome, diabetes and kidney disease; stage 1 includes fasting glucose 100-124 or HbA1c 5.7-6.4; screening advises annual assessment of MetS components (BP, triglycerides, HDL-C, glucose by fasting glucose or HbA1c) for stage 2 or higher, with non-fasting lipids acceptable. Insulin resistance appears as mechanism ("excess or dysfunctional adipose tissue ... can cause inflammation, insulin resistance") and as a reason for intensified lifestyle change in MetS, with no assay. This closes the "AHA 2023 CKM advisory could not be retrieved" item to excerpt level.

D. IRIS secondary analyses the team does not cite: Viscoli 2017 (stroke; subgroup text above), the IRIS HF analysis (PMID 29934374; its excerpted table lists "HOMA-IR >6.2 (upper quartile)" with a risk ratio 0.95 and P = 0.82, where I infer the column is HF risk, so top-quartile HOMA-IR did not predict HF within IRIS; column meaning inferred, check the paper), and the diabetes-prevention paper (PMID 27465265; diabetes in 3.8% vs 7.7%, HR 0.48, 0.33 to 0.69; baseline mean HOMA-IR 5.4, insulin 22.4 microunits/mL; HOMA-IR after one year 4.1 vs 5.7). Relevance: (1) a distribution check on the "near the cutoff" argument: with an interquartile range of 3.8 to 6.2, about a quarter of enrolled people were between 3.0 and 3.8, so the near-threshold misclassification concern applies to a minority of enrollees but still to a real share (the file's "3.0 to about 4.5" range is plausible; I did not compute the share); (2) safety (heart failure).

E. Heart failure. FINEARTS-HF eGDR (PMID 40916719) and the single-centre HFpEF study (PMID 37364818). A third source seen only as a search-result page: PMC9107647 (published 2022; HF patients without diabetes, overweight or hypertension; IR by HOMA-IR in 55.3% vs 26.3% of controls; HOMA-IR cutoff 2.53 taken from the top quartile of the control group). PMID and DOI not retrieved. Its use here is limited to one point: a cutoff of 2.53 from a local control group differs from IRIS's 3.0, which supports the project's assay and population-specific cutoff statement.

F. Guideline text on pioglitazone safety (2022 HF guideline, section 7.3.7 and 6.1): see row 7.

G. Searches that found nothing. Not found in the retrieved excerpts: (1) any recommendation in PREVENT, the 2026 dyslipidaemia guideline, the CKM advisory or ESC 2021 to measure fasting insulin, HOMA-IR or TyG; (2) a randomised trial of pioglitazone or another agent in cardiology stratified by HOMA-IR other than IRIS (search strings in section 5; result sets were not screened beyond the top results, so this is a weak negative). A search for TyG added to PREVENT returned no usable result (see log).

## 3. Risks of overreach in the project's wording

1. "No established clinical utility" lists non-cardiology populations only. A cardiology reader can think the verdict was not tested for risk assessment, which is the largest use case. The text should say risk prediction and treatment selection explicitly.
2. "Shown not to add value" (about 40% fasting insulin and HOMA-IR; about 30% TyG) mixes the claim "studies found no gain" with "adequate studies exist". The files name nulls in WHI, Hu, NHANES, Health ABC and Framingham, none of them tested over PREVENT or SCORE2 with decision thresholds. Keep the low figures; do not let readers move from "not shown to add value" to "shown to add no value".
3. "IRIS shows pioglitazone helps ..." should keep "in people without diabetes, no heart failure, with prior stroke or TIA, HOMA-IR above 3.0 on a central laboratory assay". It is a selection by a threshold in one trial; it does not show that HOMA-IR is the best selector, and the HOMA subgroup P value is not confirmed.
4. "The IRIS entry criterion is already embedded in guideline wording" (bottom line 3): true for ADA and AACE; the AHA/ASA 2021 guideline discusses IRIS but I could not confirm a formal recommendation; do not add it to the list of guidelines that recommend pioglitazone until its recommendation table is read.
5. eGDR is not insulin-resistance measurement by assay. Writing "eGDR-based analyses" in a list of IR evidence can be read as support for insulin assays. Keep the measure label (clinical-variable surrogate).
6. The MR bullet should not be used as evidence for or against any measurement; it is causal evidence for a phenotype that includes lipids.
7. Do not describe the 2018 cholesterol guideline as current.
