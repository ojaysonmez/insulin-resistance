# Clinical utility of insulin resistance (IR) measurement: endocrinologist assessment

Role: ir-endocrinologist. Independent run. Coverage/reimbursement out of scope.
Citation policy: items tagged [V] had their PMID/title/DOI retrieved from PubMed in this run (abstract read where stated). Items tagged [U] are statements from clinical knowledge/expert practice that I could NOT tie to a verified citation in this run; treat as unverified. No citation was invented.

## Bottom line
Outside a few narrow niches, measuring IR (fasting insulin, HOMA-IR, OGTT-insulin, TyG) does not change what a specialist does. Management is driven by glycaemia, weight, BMI/waist, liver fibrosis scores, lipids, BP and phenotype. IR is best seen as a mechanism and research construct. Clinically actionable niches: (1) lipodystrophy / severe IR syndromes, (2) selecting an IR-targeted drug (pioglitazone) in non-diabetic high-risk patients (one trial used HOMA-IR >3.0 as entry criterion), (3) hypoglycaemia / hyperinsulinism work-up (insulin/C-peptide; a different question from IR), (4) risk stratification adjunct, not a decision driver.

## Test limits (apply to every condition)
- Gold standard is the hyperinsulinemic-euglycemic clamp [V] PMID 382871 (DeFronzo 1979, DOI 10.1152/ajpendo.1979.237.3.E214); impractical in routine care.
- HOMA-IR [V] PMID 3899825 (Matthews 1985, DOI 10.1007/BF00280883) is derived from fasting glucose and insulin; assumes steady state and intact beta-cell function [U].
- Repeatability: in 102 older adults without diabetes, within-person CV was 30.4% for HOMA-IR and 28.8% for insulin; ICC 0.70; minimum detectable change 2.3 [V] PMID 29618016 (DOI 10.1210/jc.2017-02437; abstract read). A single value is a noisy basis for a treatment decision.
- HOMA-IR, QUICKI and FIRI are one-to-one transformations of one another; correlation with the clamp alone is insufficient evidence of clinical utility [V] PMID 29940866 (DOI 10.1186/s12874-018-0521-y, abstract read); see also PMID 20545928 (DOI 10.1111/j.1651-2227.2010.01911.x).
- No universal HOMA-IR cutoff: thresholds vary with age, sex and race [V] PMID 35939943 (DOI 10.1016/j.dsx.2022.102581; abstract read; narrative review, literature search Oct 2020).
- TyG: simple surrogate compared with clamp [V] PMID 20484475 (Guerrero-Romero 2010, DOI 10.1210/jc.2010-0288). Association with ASCVD: meta-analysis of 8 cohorts [V] PMID 33812373 (DOI 10.1186/s12933-021-01268-9; abstract read); other TyG meta-analyses PMID 35778731 (DOI 10.1186/s12933-022-01546-0) and PMID 37415168 (DOI 10.1186/s12933-023-01906-4) [titles verified only]. Association is not proof of incremental decision value.
- Insulin assay harmonisation is incomplete: interlaboratory comparison of antibody-free LC-MS/MS for insulin and C-peptide [V] PMID 38549041 (DOI 10.1093/clinchem/hvae034, title only) and commutability work on insulin harmonisation [V] PMID 36750430 (DOI 10.1177/00045632231159291, title only). I did not retrieve numeric inter-assay bias; listed as open issue.
- Other limits [U]: 8+ h fast needed and sample handling affects insulin; HOMA invalid with exogenous insulin and unreliable with beta-cell failure (advanced T2D); insulin cross-reactivity with analogues/proinsulin varies by assay.
- Population differences: South Asian adults develop T2D at lower BMI; see Dutch South Asian 14-year cohort [V, title only] PMID 40353919 (DOI 10.1007/s00592-025-02513-3). Cutoffs from one group can misclassify another.

## Per condition

### 1. Type 2 diabetes
- Use case: subtype/drug selection, progression prediction.
- What IR adds: data-driven clusters (severe insulin-resistant diabetes SIRD, severe insulin-deficient SIDD; derived using HOMA2-B and HOMA2-IR [U]) differ in complications [V] PMID 29503172 (Ahlqvist 2018, DOI 10.1016/S2213-8587(18)30051-2). In ADOPT, simple features (age at diagnosis; baseline renal function) predicted progression as well as or better than clusters [V] PMID 31047901 (Dennis 2019, DOI 10.1016/S2213-8587(19)30087-7; abstract partly read). Reproducibility of subtypes by machine learning [V, title only] PMID 39168869 (DOI 10.1007/s00125-024-06248-8).
- Decision it would change: little. Drug choice rests on CV/renal/weight comorbidity and glycaemia; I would not order insulin/HOMA-IR to pick metformin vs GLP-1 RA vs SGLT2i. C-peptide (not IR) is the practical test when diabetes type or insulin need is unclear [U].
- Limits: HOMA-IR invalid on exogenous insulin and in late beta-cell failure.
- Evidence strength: low for changing decisions; moderate for prognostic association. Status: research use.

### 2. Prediabetes / diabetes prevention
- Use case: who to treat with metformin vs lifestyle.
- Evidence: DPP showed lifestyle and metformin reduced incident diabetes vs placebo [V] PMID 11832527 (DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa012512); magnitudes (58%, 31%) from memory [U]. Entry was by glucose criteria and BMI, not IR. I found no verified trial in which IR-stratified allocation improved outcomes.
- What IR adds: pathophysiological phenotyping (IR-predominant vs secretion defect), plausible but unproven for tailoring.
- Decision changed: none in guideline practice; glucose, HbA1c, OGTT and BMI drive action; lifestyle first-line regardless of IR [U].
- Evidence strength: low for use of IR.

### 3. Non-diabetic atherosclerotic disease (pioglitazone niche)
- IRIS: non-diabetic patients with recent stroke/TIA and HOMA-IR >3.0 randomised to pioglitazone vs placebo; stroke/MI HR 0.76 (95% CI 0.62-0.93); incident diabetes HR 0.48; more weight gain, oedema, fracture [V] PMID 26886418 (Kernan 2016, DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa1506930; abstract read).
- The one verified case where an IR measure defined the treated population. Caveat: all participants were IR-selected, so IRIS does not show IR is needed to benefit (no non-IR arm). Not adopted as routine HOMA-IR screening [U].
- Evidence strength: moderate for effect; low for IR as predictive biomarker of benefit.

### 4. PCOS
- Use case: metabolic risk, treatment choice, diagnosis.
- Guideline: 2023 International Evidence-based Guideline [V] PMID 37580314 (DOI 10.1210/clinem/dgad463; abstract read; it does not state the IR-testing recommendation). From memory [U]: IR is not used for diagnosis and routine IR testing is not recommended because available tests are not clinically validated in PCOS; glycaemic assessment (OGTT/HbA1c) and cardiometabolic screening are advised instead. Verify against full text.
- Inositol evidence for the 2023 update [V, title only] PMID 38163998 (DOI 10.1210/clinem/dgad762).
- Decision changed: IR would not change Rotterdam diagnosis; I treat on phenotype (cycles, hirsutism, fertility goal, weight, glycaemia). I did not verify a trial showing baseline HOMA-IR predicts metformin response (search returned nothing usable).
- Evidence strength: low for IR measurement; expert opinion favours not testing.

### 5. MASLD / MASH
- Use case: fibrosis case-finding, therapy choice.
- Guidelines: EASL-EASD-EASO 2024 executive summary [V] PMID 38869512 (DOI 10.1007/s00125-024-06196-3; abstract read) and full guideline PMID 38852583 (DOI 10.1159/000539371). Abstract: FIB-4 then elastography in people with cardiometabolic risk factors, particularly T2D or obesity; lifestyle, incretin-based therapy, bariatric surgery, and resmetirom for non-cirrhotic MASH with fibrosis stage >=2 where approved. IR measurement is not in the stated algorithm. AASLD guidance [V] PMID 36727674 (DOI 10.1097/HEP.0000000000000323; abstract unavailable, so its IR content is not stated here).
- Decision changed: none from HOMA-IR; fibrosis stage and T2D/obesity status direct care. Pioglitazone as an IR-targeting option in MASH with T2D is from memory [U]; no verified trial retrieved.
- Evidence strength: high that fibrosis staging, not IR, drives management; low for IR measurement.

### 6. Obesity-related metabolic disease / metabolic syndrome
- IR proposed as the underlying mechanism [V] PMID 35939943. Obesity-drug and surgery eligibility is BMI/comorbidity-based [U]; insulin/HOMA-IR not used for eligibility or titration.
- Evidence strength: low. May help phenotype hyperinsulinaemia; unproven to change therapy.

### 7. Type 1 diabetes with IR features ("double diabetes")
- Estimated glucose disposal rate (eGDR; from waist, BP, HbA1c [U]) is a clinical IR proxy needing no insulin assay. Associations: eGDR and first MI in diabetes [V, title only] PMID 42178592 (DOI 10.1186/s40842-026-00294-4); IR and sudomotor dysfunction in T1D [V, title only] PMID 41257477 (DOI 10.1530/EC-25-0657). Insulin-based HOMA is invalid in T1D.
- Evidence strength: low (observational); research use.

### 8. Lipodystrophy and severe IR syndromes
- Diagnosis by clinical phenotype plus genetics; annual screening for diabetes, dyslipidaemia, liver/kidney/heart disease; metreleptin effective in generalised forms [V] PMID 27710244 (Brown 2016 multi-society guideline, DOI 10.1210/jc.2016-2466; abstract read). Update: position statement [V, title only] PMID 41713690 (DOI 10.1016/j.eprac.2026.02.014).
- Marked hyperinsulinaemia supports diagnosis and monitoring [U]; meaningful use but very small population.
- Evidence strength: moderate (guideline, largely expert opinion).

### 9. Hyperinsulinaemic hypoglycaemia
- Insulin and C-peptide during fasting hypoglycaemia are standard for insulinoma/hyperinsulinism [U]. This is hypersecretion, distinct from IR.

## Established vs opinion vs research
- Established: clamp as reference; glycaemia-based diagnosis of prediabetes/T2D and PCOS screening; fibrosis-first MASLD pathway; insulin/C-peptide in hypoglycaemia and lipodystrophy.
- Expert opinion: IR phenotyping to tailor therapy; TyG as screening aid.
- Research: IR/secretion-based diabetes subtypes; IR-guided drug allocation (except IRIS-type selection).

## Open questions
1. Does IR-stratified prevention (pioglitazone, metformin) beat glucose/BMI-based allocation? No verified trial found.
2. Does baseline HOMA-IR add predictive value beyond BMI, waist, TG/HDL, glucose, HbA1c (reclassification)?
3. What are assay-harmonised, ethnicity-specific insulin/HOMA-IR reference ranges?
4. Does IR (or eGDR) identify who benefits from pioglitazone or incretins outside IRIS?
5. Confirm full-text PCOS 2023 statement on IR testing.
6. Search limitations: PubMed tool returned few results for many queries; no Consensus or ClinicalTrials.gov searches run; DPP effect sizes, PCOS IR statement and several test-limit points are [U].

## Evidence strength summary
- IR measurement changing diagnosis/treatment in T2D, prediabetes, PCOS, MASLD, obesity: low.
- IR as prognostic marker (CV, diabetes): moderate (observational/meta-analytic), not decision-changing.
- IR-selected pioglitazone in non-diabetic post-stroke: moderate (single large RCT).
- Lipodystrophy/severe IR: moderate, narrow population.
- Test reliability limits: moderate (verified repeatability and cutoff-variability data).
