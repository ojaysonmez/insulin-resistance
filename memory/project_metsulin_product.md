- The Metsulin device measures **insulin, C-peptide and glucose** in capillary blood (user, 2026-10-25). Do not list glucose as a missing analyte or recommend adding it; the open question is the glucose channel's accuracy for each use (for example laboratory-method glucose for hypoglycaemia criteria).
- It does not measure proinsulin, urine C-peptide, BOHB, antibodies or drug screens.
- It is home-based; a CLIA point-of-care version is considered as a second angle.
- Threshold: a home or point-of-care test makes sense only if a person needs it about 5-10 or more times a year; below that a central laboratory test is enough.
- Group findings on four axes: frequency (one-time, occasional, frequent), analyte (C-peptide, insulin), timing (fasting, post-meal, random, timed at hypoglycaemia), setting (home, clinic). See `usecases/scoring/metsulin-fit.md` section 1a.
