# V19 skeptic review: rows 14, 9, 28, 12, 21, 46, 35 (units V14 to V18)

Role: skeptic. Date 2026-10-23. Machine-readable findings: `usecases/scoring/V19.json`. Coverage and reimbursement are out of scope. Measure type is named in each finding. Source text I could not open is listed at the end.

## Verdict
The seven rows are scored with mostly sound sources, and every PMID I checked (44 resolved, none flagged as retracted) matches its cited claim except for the points below, but the set is not ready to enter the ranking unchanged. Four items must be fixed: row 9 mixes two decisions across units and D3 = 3 reads PancreasFest statement 2.4 as naming C-peptide with the wrong agreement figure (45 percent, not 58); row 21 D7 is scored 3 although its own numbers give about 9,500 (bin 2); row 46 D4 = 3 ignores that the criteria require a stimulated or 24-hour urine value and is inconsistent with rows 9 and 28. Several should-fix items change a ranking position: D2 is applied inconsistently (row 14 = 1, rows 28 and 35 = 2 on weaker, off-population evidence); the D7 chains for rows 14, 12, 28 and 46 hinge on unsourced shares (rows 14 and 12 cross bins; clarification 21 asks for ranges that are missing); row 46 and row 12 mix Japanese criteria with US counts; PV entries bundle pieces and conflict on effort. Differences between roles scoring the same dimension (V17 vs V18, D1, D5, D6, S) are all 1 point or less (row 14 S 0/1; row 12 D5 1/2 and D6 3/2; row 46 D1 3/2; row 35 D1 0/1); none exceeds the threshold. For the four that matter I give the supported reading above. Anchor clarifications 1 (prevalent pool, row 28), 3 (row 21), 4 (row 21), 5, 13, 14, 16 (row 12 V17), 20 (row 46) and 21 were not applied cleanly; 6, 12, 17, 18, 19, 22 and 24 were applied. Overall the evidence for these seven uses is weak at the decision level: no row has D2 above 2, the only guideline-level support (row 14, 'may be considered', LoE 3) shows no management change, and the strongest uses on stakes (rows 12, 21) rest on case reports and national criteria. Counts for the US are low-confidence in six of seven rows. Not checked by me: StatPearls, CDC NDSR, CDC KDSS and the Diabetes Care DKA page (blocked from this environment); the AASLD 2023 negative and the Imagawa criteria text beyond the abstract (taken from project files).

### 2. [must fix] row 9, D3
V14 reads PancreasFest Guidance Statement 2.4 as naming C-peptide and reports '58 percent top agreement'. Checked in PMC3830751: the statement reads 'Assessment of pancreatic endocrine reserve, and importantly that of functional beta-cell mass, should be performed as part of the evaluation and followup for TPIAT'. It names the function, not the analyte; C-peptide appears only in the evidence paragraph ('can be estimated from serum C-peptide levels determined during either oral glucose tolerance ... or mixed-nutrient meal testing'). The agreement for 2.4 is A 45%, B 20%, C 10%, D 18%, E 8%; the 58% figure belongs to statement 1.1. Under clarification 5 (a recommendation that names the condition or function, not the measurement, scores 2) D3 is 2, and the confidence statement 'consensus with 58 percent top agreement' is wrong.

Fix: Set D3 to 2 for 9b (3 only if the reason quotes text that names C-peptide in the recommendation itself), correct the agreement figure to 45% A (26% in D or E), and note that the same paper's statement 2.3 uses an absent pancreatic polypeptide response, not C-peptide, as the specific indicator of type 3c diabetes.

### 4. [must fix] row 46, D4
