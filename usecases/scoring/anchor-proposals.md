# Anchor proposals (APPROVED by the user 2026-10-19 and applied to the rubric)

> Paths: this file lives in `usecases/scoring/`. It collects the anchor problems reported by units U1, S1, S2, S3 and S4 (all in their `anchor_issues` fields) and proposes a clarification for each. The proposals were approved as drafted and are now in the rubric under 'Anchor clarifications'.

| # | Dimension | Problem reported | Reported by | Proposed clarification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | D7 | For chronic conditions (post-bariatric hypoglycaemia, diabetes classification) the number of patients is a prevalent pool, not new cases per year | U1 | Count new cases per year plus the share of the existing pool that is evaluated or retested in a year; write the share as a stated assumption |
| 2 | D7 | A row can be read broadly (all newly diagnosed adults, bin 5) or narrowly (the guideline indication, bin 4) | U1 | Use the narrowest population in which the guideline or study applies the test; state the broad reading as a note |
| 3 | D7 | Range spans two bins | U1, S1, S2 | Score the bin that contains the point estimate; mark confidence low when the range spans bins; and check that the score equals the bin of the stated number |
| 4 | D7 | The anchor counts situations, not diagnoses, so the work-up population is larger than disease incidence | S4 | Count patients in whom the clinical question arises (for example everyone worked up for hypoglycaemia), not only those with the final diagnosis |
| 5 | D3 | A guideline that recommends the drug but not the measurement (pioglitazone after stroke) | U1, S1 | D3 is about the measurement: score 2 if a guideline names the condition that the test identifies but not the test, 3 only if it recommends or requires the measurement |
| 6 | D3 | Single-country expert criteria (Japanese criteria) have no anchor; whether a US-relevant score applies | S1, S2 | Score status for the US setting; national criteria elsewhere count as 1 (not addressed in the US) unless a US or international body adopts them, and are noted |
| 7 | D3 | A standard diagnostic step in a guideline: 4 or 3 | S4 | 4 only when the guideline or label requires the test for the decision; a recommended step is 3 |
| 8 | D2 | A trial entry criterion that never tested measurement-guided selection | U1, S1 | Score 2 at most: the evidence shows benefit in selected patients, not that selecting by the measurement is better |
| 9 | D2 | Regulatory approval on a surrogate endpoint (teplizumab) does not fit anchor 4 | S4 | Score 3 for approval on a surrogate, 4 only for hard-outcome randomized evidence for the decision |
| 10 | D2 | Endpoint-validity evidence (trial endpoints) does not fit the decision-impact wording | S3 | For research-endpoint uses score the evidence that the measure is an accepted surrogate (2 for cohort evidence, 3 for a consensus statement) and say so |
| 11 | D1 | The line between counselling only (1) and chooses among accepted options (2) is unclear for prognosis uses (type 1 staging, remission prediction) | S1 | Prognosis that sets monitoring intensity or counselling is 1; prognosis that selects a treatment or a diagnostic step is 2 |
| 12 | D1 | Anchor 4 (required step or eligibility criterion) is ambiguous for national diagnostic criteria | S2 | 4 only where the criterion is a legal or regulatory requirement for the decision in the scoring country; national diagnostic criteria elsewhere are 3 |
