## Reasoning
- **Row 51.** PMID 34531293, DOI 10.2337/db21-0526 (medRxiv preprint text, keyword-read per row). One RCT (n=1105; 908 completers) with phenotype-stratified lifestyle; high-risk group favoured intensified lifestyle (CI -0.544 to -0.036). Surrogate glucose endpoint, no replication, not insulin-only, no guideline. D2 = 2 (3 is the alternative). D8 = 1: no comparison against glucose, HbA1c, BMI or liver-fat scores in the row; mechanism alone is capped at 2 (clarification 19).
- **Row 52.** PMID 35120288, DOI 10.4088/jcp.21m14022 (n=45; all IR at entry, so entry criterion only, clarification 8); PMID 26602230, DOI 10.1016/j.psychres.2015.10.013 (42 enrolled, subgroup finding). Neither randomised by IR status. D2 = 1, D8 = 1.
- **Row 53.** PMID 29466592, DOI 10.1001/jama.2018.0245 (n=609, interaction P=.47). The one trial found no effect modification, so D2 = 0 and D8 = 0. The score is about the insulin test, not about diet.
- **Row 54.** PMID 11773152 (10.1200/jco.2002.20.1.42), PMID 21115858 (10.1200/jco.2009.26.4473), PMID 22162568 (10.1200/jco.2011.36.2723), PMID 34103538 (10.1038/s41523-021-00275-z), PMID 30051890 (10.1038/s41586-018-0343-4; mouse; erratum 10.1038/s41586-018-0506-3). Associations, BMI-attenuated in one cohort (HR 1.7, 0.9 to 3.1), no insulin-guided decision tested. D2 = 1. D8 is null because no source compares insulin with stage, receptor status or BMI.

## Path to viability
All four need large-effort evidence (trial-level): 51 replication with a simplified phenotype and hard outcome; 52 IR-stratified randomised trial with clinical mood outcome; 53 a contradicting second trial or pooled interaction analysis (otherwise closed); 54 an insulin-stratified trial with recurrence outcome plus incremental-value analysis over stage, receptor status and BMI. Details in `usecases/scoring/V29.json`.
