## Row 5

## Row 13
- D1 2: HOMA-IR above 3.0 was the IRIS entry criterion (Kernan 2016, PMID 26886418, DOI 10.1056/nejmoa1506930, abstract plus full text via row 13). ADA 2026 Rec 3.13 and AACE 2022 R 9.4 (PMID 35963508, DOI 10.1016/j.eprac.2022.08.002) say pioglitazone may/should be considered with insulin resistance and prediabetes, with no assay or threshold. Anchor clarification 8 caps D2 at 2 for entry criteria; for D1 the measurement chooses among accepted options.
- D5 2: fasting draw is easy but HOMA-IR is not a routine panel and the assay is not standardised.
- D6 2: a false-positive value exposes the patient to pioglitazone harms (weight gain over 4.5 kg in 52.2%).
- S 3: recurrent stroke or MI, 9.0% against 11.8%.
