## Row 6 (random C-peptide)
- D2 = 3. Consensus-backed (Holt 2021, PMID 34590174, DOI 10.1007/s00125-021-05568-3, abstract; Jones and Hattersley 2013, PMID 23413806, DOI 10.1111/dme.12159, abstract). No controlled outcome study of C-peptide-guided changes found in the screened records; C-peptide is mostly the reference standard in accuracy studies (PMID 31558459, DOI 10.1136/bmjopen-2019-031586).
- D8 = 3. Clinical-only models are already good (AUC 0.95 vs histology; 0.97 with antibodies and genetic risk score, PMID 32634859, DOI 10.1111/dme.14361; AUC 0.95-0.98 in youth, PMID 38252849, DOI 10.2337/dc23-1815; accuracy 68-88%, PMID 36368478, DOI 10.1016/j.jclinepi.2022.10.022). Antibodies are first line. C-peptide adds in the subgroup where insulin deficiency itself must be known (insulin-treated, over 3 years). Clinical features were unhelpful in ambiguous South Asian cases (PMID 39805939, DOI 10.1038/s41598-024-80348-8).

## Row 5 (insulin/C-peptide at hypoglycaemia)
- D2 = 1. Reviews and case series only (PMID 30101281, DOI 10.1210/jc.2018-00528; PMID 42703785, DOI 10.1530/ec-26-0480; PMID 42849663, DOI 10.1016/j.eprac.2026.09.119; CGM prevalence PMID 34802853, DOI 10.1016/j.numecd.2021.08.047). No accepted insulin threshold; no accuracy or decision-impact study found in the screened records.
- D8 = 2 (low confidence). Glucose, CGM and history define the syndrome; insulin/C-peptide matter to exclude insulinoma, exogenous insulin or drugs.

## Row 13 (HOMA-IR above 3.0, pioglitazone)
- D2 = 2. IRIS (PMID 26886418, DOI 10.1056/nejmoa1506930) HR 0.76 (0.62-0.93) but HOMA-IR was an entry criterion only (anchor clarification 8: maximum 2). Guidelines cite IRIS and name no assay or threshold.
- D8 = 1 (low). No contrast with patients lacking IR; diabetes-prevention effect concentrated in raised fasting glucose or HbA1c (PMID 27465265, DOI 10.2337/dc16-0798). AACE accepts prediabetes or T2D as triggers.

## Path to viability
- Row 6: controlled cohort/registry of C-peptide-guided reclassification with treatment-switch outcomes and assay harmonisation. Type evidence, medium.
- Row 5: diagnostic-accuracy study with derived and externally validated insulin/C-peptide thresholds against CGM and clinical course. Type evidence, medium.
- Row 13: IRIS re-analysis testing HOMA-IR and glucose-based alternatives as effect modifiers. Type evidence, small (a new trial in low-HOMA-IR stroke patients would be large).
