## Reasoning and sources per row

**Row 31.** D1 3: the C-peptide decides stopping or avoiding insulin, but antibodies and clinical course substitute in part (clarification 15). D5 3: a PCP can order a random C-peptide with a glucose in a routine draw. D6 2: burden 4, false-result consequence 2 (withdrawal on a misread result risks hyperglycaemia or DKA; the row says never stop insulin without the check). S 2: default is continued insulin, with moderate harm. Sources: `usecases-verified.md` row 31 (Holt 2021, Jones and Hattersley 2013, ADA Standards 2026 s2 PMID 41358893 DOI 10.2337/dc26-s002, all as recorded; Funakoshi 2011 PMID 24843502 DOI 10.1111/j.2040-1124.2010.00096.x abstract). Confidence medium (D1, D5), low (D6, S).

**Row 7.** D1 2: C-peptide or UCPCR is one triage component (PMID 37794142 DOI 10.1038/s43856-023-00369-8; PMID 40640995 DOI 10.5694/mja2.52717; full text as recorded). D5 2: mostly endocrine or genetics, post-meal UCPCR collection not routine. D6 3: burden 3, consequence 3, with a quoted PPV of 20% for the pathway. S 2: missed MODY means insulin where a sulfonylurea works, plus missed cascade testing. Confidence medium for D1 and D6, low otherwise.

**Row 3.** D1 2: insulin and C-peptide both high do not separate sulfonylurea hypoglycaemia from insulinoma; the drug screen discriminates (row 3 correction). D5 2: needs a documented hypoglycaemic sample or supervised fast. D6 2: burden 2, consequence 2 (the screen may miss newer agents, no accuracy data retrieved). S 3: without the pattern, recurrent severe hypoglycaemia continues or an insulinoma search follows. Sources: row 3 (Cryer 2009 abstract; PES 2015 full text, PMID 25957977 DOI 10.1016/j.jpeds.2015.03.057). Confidence medium (D1, D5), low otherwise.

**Row 4.** D1 2: insulin is supportive, not required; BOHB, FFA and glucagon response are the reliable markers (HI guideline 2023, PMID 37454648 DOI 10.1159/000531766, full text as recorded; PES 2015 as above). D5 2: critical sample in hospital by paediatrics, then paediatric endocrinology. D6 2: burden 3, consequence 2 (undetectable insulin falsely reassures; antibody interference, row 25). S 3: the disease causes permanent brain injury, but marginal harm without insulin is lower because other markers exist. Stefanovski 2022 (PMID 35416981 DOI 10.2337/dc21-2009) is a drug-trial endpoint, not diagnostic. Confidence medium (D1, D5), low otherwise.

**Row 10.** D1 3: C-peptide is one of four Igls criteria that classify the graft (PMID 29528967 DOI 10.1097/tp.0000000000002158; PMID 29453879 DOI 10.1111/tri.13138; PMID 34061967 DOI 10.1210/clinem/dgab386; abstracts as recorded, Igls 2018 full text read by the skeptic per row 10). D5 2: transplant centre. D6 3: burden 3 (fasting plus mixed-meal), consequence 3 (kidney dysfunction raises C-peptide; three other criteria correct errors). S 2: graft status judged without C-peptide risks delayed decisions on insulin and immunosuppression. Confidence medium (D1), high (D5), low otherwise.

**Row 32.** D1 3: post-DKA C-peptide with antibodies determines insulin withdrawal at about 12 months; antibodies substitute in part (Jones and Hattersley 2013 as recorded; PMID 17130187 DOI 10.2337/dc06-0749 abstract). D5 2: endocrinology follow-up, stimulated test. D6 2: burden 3, consequence 2 (early or falsely high result then withdrawal risks recurrent DKA; not within about 2 weeks of the acute episode). S 2: default lifelong insulin; moderate harm. Confidence medium (D1, D5), low otherwise.

**Row 47.** D1 3 under clarification 12 (national criteria elsewhere are 3); a US PCP reading would be 2. Source: PMID 38184802 DOI 10.1111/jdi.14121, full text read by the skeptic as recorded. D5 2: specialist classification. D6 3: burden 4, consequence 3. S 2: delayed insulin start with moderate ketoacidosis risk. Confidence low throughout (single national source, outside the US).

**Row 49.** D1 1: prognosis sets monitoring intensity, not treatment (clarification 11; row 30 covers treatment selection). Sources: Ismail 2022 PMID 35998266 DOI 10.2337/dc22-0167; Insel 2015 PMID 26404926 DOI 10.2337/dc15-1419 (descriptive, no recommendation to test); ADA Standards 2026 s2 covers autoantibody screening only. D5 1: 5 to 9 timed samples, research-network protocol. D6 2: burden 3, consequence 2 (thresholds are cohort-specific medians; Galderisi 2025 PMID 39992257 DOI 10.1210/clinem/dgaf086 found AUC C-peptide not significant in the multivariable model; Sims 2026 PMID 42684379 DOI 10.2337/dc26-1111 abstract only). S 2. Confidence medium (D1, D5), low otherwise. Measure type is post-load C-peptide, not insulin.
