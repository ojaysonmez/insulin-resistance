## Reasoning per row

**Row 31 (origin: both).** D2 = 3. Jones and Hattersley 2013 (PMID 23413806, DOI 10.1111/dme.12159, abstract) say the key role of C-peptide is classification and management of insulin-treated patients, with greatest utility 3-5 years after diagnosis. The ADA/EASD adult T1D consensus (PMID 34593612, DOI 10.2337/dci21-0043, abstract) and ADA Standards 2026 section 2 (PMID 41358893, DOI 10.2337/dc26-s002, abstract; content from project file) are consensus sources. The only retrieved study near the stop-insulin decision, Funakoshi 2011 (PMID 24843502, DOI 10.1111/j.2040-1124.2010.00096.x, abstract), is retrospective, 201 Japanese type 2 patients, with likelihood ratios 1.6-2.8 (CPI sensitivity 61 percent, specificity 78 percent); it addresses who needs insulin to reach target, not safe withdrawal. So classification is consensus-backed (3) and the de-escalation step alone would be 2. D8 = 2. Alternatives (clinical features, antibodies, genetic risk score) were modelled with C-peptide as the outcome definition, not as a predictor (PMID 31558459, DOI 10.1136/bmjopen-2019-031586, abstract), so incremental value over them was not quantified. Anchor 19 caps mechanism-only at 2.

**Row 7 (both).** D2 = 3. The PMDI systematic review with expert recommendations (PMID 37794142, DOI 10.1038/s43856-023-00369-8, abstract) and the ANZ 2025 consensus (PMID 40640995, DOI 10.5694/mja2.52717, abstract; recommends testing when probability is high using validated screening tools) back triage. UCPCR studies: adults, 120 min after the largest meal, cutoff 0.2 nmol/mmol separating HNF1A/4A MODY from T1D over 5 years, AUC 0.98, 97 percent sensitivity and 96 percent specificity (PMID 21270186, DOI 10.2337/dc10-1293, abstract); children, 0.7 nmol/mmol after 2 years separates non-T1D from T1D (100 percent / 97 percent) but MODY from type 2 only AUC 0.57 (PMID 23289766, DOI 10.1111/pedi.12008, abstract); a 69-patient Turkish series, UCPCR 0.22 (PMID 25792383, DOI 10.1159/000375410, abstract). No prospective yield or outcome trial. D8 = 3. Updated probability calculator (PMID 41086125, DOI 10.2337/dc25-1029, abstract truncated): in early-insulin-treated people clinical features alone ROC AUC 0.80 (95% CrI 0.71-0.82), with C-peptide and antibodies 0.98 (0.95-0.98), biomarkers alone 0.96, validated in an independent cohort of 1,025. C-peptide not separated from antibodies; UCPCR cannot separate MODY from type 2 diabetes.

**Row 3 (both).** D2 = 2. The Endocrine Society guideline (PMID 19088155, DOI 10.1210/jc.2008-1410, abstract) recommends measuring glucose, insulin, C-peptide, proinsulin, beta-hydroxybutyrate and circulating oral hypoglycaemic agents during an episode; GRADE evidence is stated as very low to moderate. `usecases-verified.md` row 3 records that the insulin/C-peptide pattern is identical to insulinoma and that the drug screen discriminates; no accuracy data retrieved (confirmed: my search for factitious/sulfonylurea hypoglycaemia accuracy found only a study guide, PMID 31194450, not opened). D8 = 1: insulin and C-peptide confirm endogenous hyperinsulinism and exclude exogenous insulin, but the sulfonylurea question is answered by the drug screen.

**Row 32 (both).** D2 = 2. Balasubramanyam 2006 (PMID 17130187, DOI 10.2337/dc06-0749, abstract truncated): 294 DKA patients followed 12-60 months; the Abeta scheme (autoantibodies plus glucagon-stimulated C-peptide at least 1.5 ng/mL) had sensitivity 99.4 percent, specificity 95.9 percent, LR+ 24.55, LR- 0.01, AUC 0.972 for absent versus preserved beta-cell function at 12 months. Maldonado 2003 (PMID 14602731, DOI 10.1210/jc.2003-030180, abstract): 103 patients, beta-negative groups had persistent insulin requirement. Review PMID 18292467 (DOI 10.1210/er.2007-0026, abstract). The accuracy outcome is defined by the same test (circular), single research network. No guideline or trial found in the screened records. D8 = 3 (low confidence): the Abeta scheme was the most accurate of four, but comparator figures were in the truncated abstract.

**Row 47 (both).** D2 = 2. The criterion (fasting C-peptide below 0.6 ng/mL as part of a definite diagnosis) is an expert definition (PMID 38184802, DOI 10.1111/jdi.14121, abstract); the derivation is not given in the abstract. Cohorts: TIDE-J, n=314, undetectable C-peptide at 5 years in 9.1 percent of slowly progressive versus 43.1 percent of acute-onset (PMID 40509916, DOI 10.2337/dc25-0579, abstract); 60 slowly progressive patients, non-progressors defined as fasting C-peptide at least 0.6 ng/mL, PPV of GADA titre rising from 56.3 to 70.0 percent and to 76.9-84.2 percent with age, BMI, C-peptide or IA-2A (PMID 38451108, DOI 10.1111/jdi.14178, abstract). D8 = 2: insulin therapy beyond 3 months is already a criterion, so C-peptide adds a severity label; its incremental value over insulin requirement was not tested.

**Row 49 (both).** D2 = 2. DPTRS (PMID 26077017, DOI 10.1007/s11892-015-0626-1, abstract) includes OGTT glucose and C-peptide sums, fasting C-peptide, age and BMI, validated externally in TrialNet; DPTRS 9.00 gave 2-year risk 0.88 (DPT-1, n=90) and 0.77 (TrialNet, n=69) (PMID 22547092, DOI 10.2337/dc12-0011, abstract). Index60 thresholds were cohort medians (PMID 35998266, DOI 10.2337/dc22-0167, abstract). Staging statement is descriptive (PMID 26404926, DOI 10.2337/dc15-1419, abstract). No recommendation to test and no study of monitoring guided by the score. D8 = 2: in DPT-1 2-year risk after dysglycaemia appeared was 0.37, so the glucose-plus-C-peptide score identifies higher risk earlier, but C-peptide was not tested as an increment over antibody count and glucose; in the n=57 model AUC C-peptide was not significant (OR 0.999, P=.330) while phi-total was (PMID 39992257, DOI 10.1210/clinem/dgaf086, abstract truncated; Table 3 from the project file). Sims 2026 (PMID 42684379, DOI 10.2337/dc26-1111) was not opened by me; abstract-level claim comes from the project file.

**Row 4 (both).** D2 = 3. The 2023 international hyperinsulinism guideline (PMID 37454648, DOI 10.1159/000531766, abstract; Rec 1.1 grade per project file) and PES 2015 (PMID 25957977, DOI 10.1016/j.jpeds.2015.03.057, no abstract in PubMed) include the critical sample. Retrospective accuracy: insulin detectable in only 23 of 28 confirmed cases, C-peptide 1.55 versus 0.11 ng/mL, glucagon rise of 30 mg/dL or more in 24 of 27 versus 0 of 30 (PMID 26490124, DOI 10.1016/j.jpeds.2015.09.045, abstract truncated); C-peptide above 291.5 pmol/L, sensitivity 82 percent and specificity 99 percent (PMID 39360602, DOI 10.1111/cen.15137, abstract truncated, single centre). D8 = 1: BOHB, FFA and glucagon response are the reliable markers; insulin and C-peptide are called less definitive.

**Row 10 (both).** D2 = 3. Igls 2018 (PMID 29528967, DOI 10.1097/tp.0000000000002158, abstract) and the 2021 symposium (PMID 34061967, DOI 10.1210/clinem/dgab386, abstract: CGM time in range improved with each Igls category in 55 recipients). CITR registry, n=677: fasting C-peptide ROC AUC 0.906 for absence of severe hypoglycaemia and 0.845 for the optimal outcome (cut point 0.33 nmol/L); MMTT stimulated C-peptide-to-glucose ratio marginally better (PMID 36657975, DOI 10.2337/dc22-1155, abstract truncated). Modified Igls in 379 TPIAT patients associated with CGM and acute C-peptide response (PMID 33020957, DOI 10.1111/tri.13762, abstract). This is prediction and classification, not decision impact. D8 = 2: no incremental analysis over HbA1c, insulin dose and CGM.

## Anchor issues

- Row 3 D2: GRADE-graded low-quality guideline fits neither anchor 2 nor 3; chose 2.
- Row 3 D8: alternative taken as the drug screen.
- Row 47 D2: national expert criterion with no stated derivation; chose 2.
- Row 10 D2: the decision is classification of graft function; prediction of outcomes was counted, decision impact is not shown.
- Row 4: the measure sits in a bundle; D2 for the bundle's backing, D8 for insulin and C-peptide alone.
- Rows 31 and 32 D8: C-peptide is itself the reference for beta-cell function, so accuracy studies are circular and anchor 19 cannot be met cleanly.
- Row 49 D8: mixed measure (glucose plus C-peptide) cannot isolate C-peptide.

## Assumptions

- Full-text claims (Holt 2021, Jones 2013, ADA Standards 2026, PMDI 2023, HI guideline, PES 2015, Igls 2018, Galderisi 2025, Sims 2026) come from `usecases-verified.md`; I read abstracts only.
- Row 31 population is adults on insulin with doubtful type or clinical T1D for 3 years or more; classification is the primary decision, de-escalation secondary.
- Truncated abstracts were treated as read only up to the truncation.
