## Row 6: C-peptide for diabetes classification

Population (narrowest, Holt 2021 and Jones and Hattersley 2013 as recorded in `usecases-verified.md` row 6): insulin-treated adults whose diabetes type is unclear and who are more than 3 years from diagnosis.

| Insulin-treated | 25% = 7.2 million | 18-32% | ASSUMPTION. Not retrieved. CDC gives only subsets: 1.8 million adults with type 1 and insulin; 3.8 million who started insulin within a year of diagnosis |

| Type unclear or misclassified | 12% = about 730,000 | 6-20% | Anchors: 14% misclassified by UK practical criteria in 601 insulin-treated adults (PMID 27080317, DOI 10.3399/bjgp16x684961, abstract; UK, not US); 11% reclassified by C-peptide in Holt 2021 (PMID 34590174, DOI 10.1007/s00125-021-05568-3, project files). The share itself is an assumption |
| Evaluated with C-peptide in a year | 5% = about 37,000 | 2-10% | ASSUMPTION. No US testing rate found (see negatives) |

Result: about 37,000 (7,000-120,000). The bin-deciding share is the annual evaluation rate, which has no source, and the range spans bins 2 to 4. Recorded as bin 2, 3 or 4, low confidence; scored bin 3 (best estimate). Broader reading: the number of insulin-treated adults with unclear type is about 730,000 (bin 4); not scored, because the clarified rule counts the evaluated share of an existing pool. Newly diagnosed adults are not added: Holt makes autoantibodies the first test at diagnosis and C-peptide valid only after 3 years.

Negative results (not found in the screened records): a US C-peptide testing rate or claims-based count; the number of US adults using insulin (total); a US estimate of misclassified diabetes type among insulin users; Medicare volume for CPT 84681.

Result: about 25,000 (5,000-77,000). The lower end crosses 10,000: bin 2 or 3, low confidence; scored bin 3. The mixed-meal test is a recognised work-up (PMID 34935944, DOI 10.1210/clinem/dgab913, abstract only: sensitivity 71% and specificity 100% for detecting hypoglycaemia in 15 patients), which supports counting meal-test patients, but no source gives the share of symptomatic patients who get one.

Negative results: no US count of patients evaluated for post-bariatric hypoglycaemia; no US claims data on glucose and insulin testing after bariatric surgery. The full text of PMID 41966914 was not read (abstract truncated before the conclusion).

## Row 13: HOMA-IR to select pioglitazone after stroke or TIA

Population: adults with recent ischemic stroke or TIA without diabetes (IRIS eligibility; ADA 2026 Rec 3.13 wording is stroke with evidence of IR and prediabetes).

| Link | Value | Range | Source or label |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ischemic strokes per year | 692,000 (795,000 x 87%) | - | CDC Stroke Facts, page dated 2025-07-10 (web). Includes about 185,000 recurrent strokes in all strokes |
| TIAs per year | 240,000 | 200,000-500,000 | AHA scientific statement on TIA in the emergency department, PMID 36655570, DOI 10.1161/str.0000000000000418 (search excerpt only; the 2009 AHA statement gives 200,000-500,000) |
| Without diabetes | 70% | 65-80% | GWTG-Stroke, PMID 29438515, DOI 10.1093/eurheartj/ehy036, abstract: 29.6% had diabetes (age 65 or older only, so the share for all ages is an assumption) |
| Not excluded (heart failure, insulin, under 40, under 14 days, death or severe disability) | 70% | 50-85% | ASSUMPTION; IRIS exclusions and the 14-day timing from IRIS full text (PMID 26886418, DOI 10.1056/nejmoa1506930, PMC4887756) |

Result: about 455,000 (300,000-800,000); bin 4 holds across the whole range. Narrower reading (also HOMA-IR above 3): IR-positive share 25-40% is an ASSUMPTION built on the IRIS statement that 3.0 marks the top quartile in people without diabetes; gives about 140,000 (75,000-320,000), also bin 4 with the low end touching bin 3. A cross-check: prediabetes affects 23-53% of non-diabetic patients with recent TIA or stroke (PMID 24993381, DOI 10.1159/000360810, abstract). The number actually measured is much lower than the number in whom the question arises; no data found on how often HOMA-IR is measured or pioglitazone started after stroke.
