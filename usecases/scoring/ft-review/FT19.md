# FT19 full-text review (papers C044, C056, C067, C082, C087, C090, C095)

Result: the full texts change no score. All 16 proposals are null (nothing to change). Several claim wordings in usecases-verified.md need correction. Proposals and quotes are in FT19.json. Local files only; no web, no git.

## Per paper
- **C044 Brown 2016 lipodystrophy guideline (row 12): confirms.** Text good. No mention of fasting insulin, hyperinsulinaemia or C-peptide. The "metabolic workup for insulin resistance" sentence is in a figure legend with no class or level, so D3 stays 2 (clarification 5). Anti-insulin-receptor (type B) syndrome is not covered, so the row label "type B IR (lipodystrophy in the guideline)" is wrong. Metreleptin criteria (leptin <4 ng/mL, HbA1c >8% and/or TG >500 mg/dL) apply to partial lipodystrophy only.
- **C056 D'Elia 2019 (rows 28, 9): adds nuance.** Text partial (PDF, two columns). Retrospective, n=87, per-patient means over years of untimed draws. Ratio about 45 (eGFR <60) vs 65; across eGFR quartiles p=0.08; no hypoglycaemia outcome measured. Says nothing on type 3c diabetes (row 9 D6 support is weak).
- **C067 Hall 2021 (row 48): adds nuance.** Text good. Insulin was measured hourly in the OGTT and analysed, and did not discriminate (hyperinsulinaemia 40% vs 31%, p=0.440; confirmed cases had lower 2-h insulin). The 55 mg/dL cut-off is attributed to a cited reference, not set by the authors. 8 of 35 controls (23%) reached it. MMTT glucose was drawn only at symptoms (17 of 40); no MMTT insulin result is reported. Supports D8 0 and D6 2.
- **C082 Stefanovski 2022 (row 4): adds nuance.** Text good. Protein-test insulin AUC rose about twofold but C-peptide AUC and modelled secretion did not change; insulin clearance fell about 30%. Glucose AUC on the protein test rose about 48% (my arithmetic), not 30%. Drug trial, 8 of 16 children completed all periods.
- **C087 Ismail 2022 (row 49): adds nuance.** Text good. Thresholds are progressor medians applied across cohorts (replication, not independent validation); AUC glucose alone gave similar 5-year risk (0.72 vs 0.75 for Index60); different C-peptide assays in the two cohorts (radioimmunoassay, Tosoh); authors point to prevention-trial selection.
- **C090 Zhou 2022 (row 28): adds nuance.** Text good. The UCPCR cut-off (<=1.13 nmol/g, AUC 0.978) uses fasting serum C-peptide <0.3 as the reference, which kidney function distorts; mean UCPCR itself differed by renal group (0.89 vs 1.67), so the cut-off largely separates the CKD group; no per-stratum accuracy. The authors' claim that UCPCR is unaffected by renal function does not match their Table 1.
- **C095 Duo 2022 (row 35): confirms.** Text good. Prospective two-centre cohort, n=1343; same-sample cut-offs 1.43-2.31 (AUC 0.69-0.80), adjusted OR 2.97, no external validation or incremental-value analysis, no insulin assay named. Authors overstate ("diagnosis of GDM can be confirmed").
