# FT28 full-text review (C077, C078, C086, C089, C092, C094, C099)

No score change is proposed. Text quality: good for C077, C078, C086, C092, C099; partial (PDF column interleaving) for C089 and C094.

## Per paper
- **C077** (PMID 34802853, DOI 10.1016/j.numecd.2021.08.047): adds nuance. All 8 studies (16 datasets, 280 non-diabetic patients, at least 1 year after surgery) measured hypoglycaemia by CGM; none measured insulin or C-peptide. The authors state there is no general consensus on diagnostic modalities and no uniform cut-off, report that provocative tests (OGTT, mixed meal) show hypoglycaemia also in healthy volunteers, cite one study where CGM (sensitivity 90%, specificity 50%) beat a mixed-meal test (33% and 40%), and say the CGM rate may be overestimated because no study paired CGM with serial plasma glucose, insulin and C-peptide. Quote: "no uniformly established cut-off values exist for diagnosis of PBH"
- **C078** (PMID 34984504, DOI 10.1007/s00268-021-06394-9): adds nuance. The guideline makes no recommendation to measure insulin, C-peptide or HOMA. On preoperative carbohydrate loading it does not recommend it: Table 2 item 7 reads 'insufficient evidence to make a recommendation' (low evidence, weak grade), and the text says there is insufficient evidence for routine use in bariatric surgery. The row wording is therefore wrong on the carbohydrate point. Quote: "Currently, there is insufficient evidence to support routine use of preoperative carbohydrate loading in bariatric surgery (Table 2)."
- **C086** (PMID 35963508, DOI 10.1016/j.eprac.2022.08.002): confirms. R 9.4 is Grade A, BEL 1 and reads 'should be considered' for insulin resistance, prediabetes or T2D with prior TIA or stroke. The evidence text describes IRIS as 'persons with insulin resistance but not DM', never mentions HOMA-IR, a threshold or any insulin assay, and adds a post hoc IRIS prediabetes subgroup (HR 0.64 for stroke) and a pooled analysis in IR, prediabetes or DM without heterogeneity. Quote: "In persons with insulin resistance, prediabetes, or T2D and a prior transient ischemic attack or stroke, pioglitazone should be considered to reduce the risk of recurrent stroke."
- **C089** (PMID 36464132, DOI 10.1016/j.eprac.2022.11.010): adds nuance. A narrative review (not a guideline) that restates the Endocrine Society 2009 approach: Whipple triad first, then insulin, C-peptide, proinsulin and beta-hydroxybutyrate on a sample taken during hypoglycaemia; a mixed-meal test 'should be considered' for suspected postprandial hypoglycaemia and is interpreted like the fasting or spontaneous pattern in its Table 3. It does not discuss idiopathic (reactive) postprandial hypoglycaemia in people without gastric surgery or give a separate meal-test insulin threshold, and says mixed-meal tests alone cannot separate insulinoma from non-insulinoma pancreatogenous hypoglycaemia. Table 3 symbols (greater-than-or-equal signs) were lost in extraction. Quote: "A mixed-meal test should be considered to provoke hypoglycemia in patients with a history suggestive of postprandial hypoglycemia."
- **C092** (PMID 36537525, DOI 10.1111/pedi.13453): confirms. Confirms row 19. Screening is the 2-h 75 g OGTT yearly from age 10, glucose at least fasting and 2 h; HbA1c is not recommended; 1-h glucose has insufficient evidence. Diagnosis uses ADA glucose criteria. Insulin appears only as pathophysiology (early-phase insulin loss in the first 30 minutes, delayed secretion and late hypoglycaemia). The word C-peptide does not occur in the text. Quote: "Screening for CFRD should be performed using the 2‐h 75 g (1.75 g/kg) OGTT."
- **C094** (PMID 36622115, DOI 10.1542/peds.2022-060640): adds nuance. The guideline is now readable (PDF extract, columns interleaved). Key action statement 6 recommends fasting plasma glucose, 2-h OGTT glucose or HbA1c for prediabetes and diabetes in children with obesity, and its exclusions line says fasting insulin is not a reliable measure of insulin resistance; the text says fasting insulin is not recommended for diagnosing prediabetes or T2DM because levels are highly variable and do not reliably correlate with insulin resistance. No C-peptide or HOMA-IR test is recommended. Quote: "Fasting insulin test is not reliable measure of insulin resistance."
- **C099** (PMID 37580314, DOI 10.1210/clinem/dgad463): confirms. Confirms. Practice points 1.9.12 and 3.1.10 say clinically available insulin assays are of limited clinical relevance and are not recommended (1.9.12) or should not be used (3.1.10) in routine care. Recommendation 1.9.9 recommends the 75-g OGTT as the most accurate glycaemic test. Practice points are defined as 'Evidence not sought', as the merged conclusion says; the Discussion adds that routinely available measures of insulin resistance are inaccurate. Quote: "clinically available insulin assays are of limited clinical relevance and should not be used in routine care"

Proposed: ERAS bariatric guideline: no recommendation to measure insulin or HOMA; on preoperative carbohydrate loading it finds insufficient evidence to make a recommendation (low evidence, weak grade). - usecases-verified.md row 16 caveat and section 4 source gaps (AAP 2023 obesity guideline, 403); claims/claims.csv UC-ABS-01, UC-GAP-09, GL-46: AAP 2023 (PMID 36622115) was marked not retrievable and 'no insulin' [recall]. The full text is now read and says more than 'no insulin testing': fasting insulin is explicitly not recommended for diagnosing prediabetes or T2DM and is not a reliable measure of insulin resistance. Proposed: AAP 2023 (PMID 36622115, DOI 10.1542/peds.2022-060640, full text, keyword search): KAS 6 recommends fasting glucose, 2-h OGTT glucose or HbA1c; its exclusions line says 'Fasting insulin test is not reliable measure of insulin resistance', and the text says fasting insulin is not recommended for diagnosis of prediabetes or T2DM. No C-peptide or HOMA-IR test is recommended. Remove the [recall] tag and the 403 gap. (C094)
- usecases-verified.md row 5 caveat 'CGM-detected hypoglycaemia common (54.3%)': Accurate but incomplete: the 54.3% is a weighted mean prevalence from CGM glucose only in 280 non-diabetic patients at least 1 year after surgery, with I2 62.8% and no insulin or C-peptide measured; the authors say it may be overestimated and that provocative tests also show hypoglycaemia in healthy volunteers. It is not a rate of patients needing a work-up. Proposed: CGM-detected low glucose in 54.3% (95% CI 44.5-63.8; 8 studies, 280 patients, I2 62.8%); no included study measured insulin or C-peptide; the authors state no uniform diagnostic cut-off exists and the CGM rate may be overestimated. (C077)
- usecases-verified.md row 48 (Palani 2023 'abstract only (context)'): Read level can be raised to full text. The review is narrative, says a mixed-meal test should be considered for suspected postprandial hypoglycaemia and applies the fasting-pattern table (insulin, C-peptide, proinsulin at glucose below 55 mg/dL) to it, but does not address idiopathic postprandial hypoglycaemia without gastric surgery, so it neither supports nor contradicts the research-only status. Proposed: Palani 2023, PMID 36464132, DOI 10.1016/j.eprac.2022.11.010, full text (narrative review, PDF extract): a mixed-meal test should be considered for suspected postprandial hypoglycaemia and is interpreted with the same insulin, C-peptide and proinsulin pattern as a fast; it does not address reactive hypoglycaemia in people without gastric surgery. (C089)
- usecases-verified.md row 13 source note ('AACE 2022 ... first 100k of 678k chars'): The local full text (about 486,000 characters) confirms R 9.4 (Grade A, BEL 1) and shows the AACE evidence text never mentions HOMA-IR or a threshold; it calls IRIS participants 'persons with insulin resistance but not DM'. It also reports a post hoc IRIS prediabetes subgroup (HR 0.64 for stroke) and pooled benefit across IR, prediabetes and diabetes, which supports that glucose-based triggers are accepted. Proposed: AACE 2022 (PMID 35963508, DOI 10.1016/j.eprac.2022.08.002), local full text, keyword search: R 9.4 (Grade A, BEL 1) 'should be considered' in insulin resistance, prediabetes or T2D with prior TIA or stroke; no insulin assay, HOMA-IR or threshold is named in the text found. (C086)
