## Candidates (ranked by promise; none looks better than tier D)
1. **IR-stratified cardiorenal drug selection (eGDR as effect modifier).** T2D with CKD, HFpEF, or stroke/TIA; eGDR (non-insulin surrogate). Papers C177, C185, C131. Post hoc RCT subgroups, weak. Nearest row 13 (pioglitazone by HOMA-IR). Needs a pre-specified interaction or a trial randomising by IR index.
2. **Early-pregnancy TyG for hypertensive disorders and adverse outcomes.** TyG (fasting, non-insulin surrogate). C188 (meta-analysis), C331 (cohort). Weak, association only. Nearest row 35(b). Needs validation and a trial showing action improves outcomes.
3. **Pre-operative prediction of post-bariatric hypoglycaemia.** C315 retrospective cohort (predictors not confirmed as insulin or C-peptide), C318, C329, C333. Weak. Nearest row 5. Needs a prospective study with post-load insulin or C-peptide.
4. **Fasting C-peptide for forecasting beta-cell depletion in established T1D.** C306 (prospective Japanese cohort), C285. Weak, no action tied. Nearest row 47. Needs validation outside Japan and a change in care.

Not found in the screened records: a pancreas-transplant candidacy use (C302, C325 contain no C-peptide text), a recommended IR test in the 2026 CKM guideline (C189), or an insulin or C-peptide use in the alpelisib consensus (C180).
