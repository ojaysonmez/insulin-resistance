## Claim issues
- `usecases-verified.md` row 1 and line 121: Cryer full text is now read; thresholds are in the guideline itself.
- Row 22: "Established" overstates "should be considered" (option-level; two small series).
- Row 23: ENETS OGTT statement is narrative; Cryer says OGTT should never be used for suspected postprandial hypoglycaemia and gives a mixed-meal test.
- Row 48: "no accepted criteria / no insulin threshold" should say a mixed-meal protocol exists, fasting criteria are applied in usage, and standards are not established.
- Row 20: PEG precipitation is not in Cryer; row 2: analogue cross-reactivity is in PES 2015 and case reports, not in Cryer.
- V3 row 3 D4 and V10 row 29 D4 reasons: the 22.2%, 13.4% and 10.8% figures are LC-MS/MS research-lab imprecision, not immunoassay disagreement.
