# FT29 full-text review (C106, C115, C128, C163, C173, C178, C182)

All seven texts read locally. Text quality: good except C163 (accepted-manuscript PDF, some garbling, supplement figure flattened) and C182 (tables 1 to 3 missing from the stored text).

## Per paper
- **C106 Shimada 2024 SPIDDM criteria (Diabetol Int copy)**: confirms. Same Japan Diabetes Society criteria as PMID 38184802; fasting C-peptide below 0.6 ng/mL, insulin need over 3 months. National criterion, fasting measure only, no accuracy data. Row 47 D3 (now 3 after FT17): no change proposed; the 3 rests on the Buzzetti consensus, not on this paper.
- **C115 SIEDP 2024**: confirms. The HOMA-IR sentence is a descriptive statement with no grade. Prediabetes and T2D screening uses glucose, HbA1c and OGTT only (supports row 36 "not evaluated"). Adds one point: regular glucose, insulin and lipid monitoring suggested in adolescent PCOS.
- **C128 Maciulewski 2026**: adds nuance. 393 adults, glucagon-stimulated C-peptide 1.35 (T1DM, LADA pooled) vs 4.6 ng/mL (T2DM); groups were defined partly by insulin need, no cut-offs or ROC, exploratory, 89 followed to 7 years (96 in one place).
- **C163 Yaghi 2018 IRIS stroke analysis**: adds nuance. Table 1 HOMA-IR confirmed (4.7 vs 4.6). The HOMA-above-median subgroup is in the supplement: HR 0.95 above, 0.69 below, interaction P 0.19. First author is Yaghi, not Viscoli.
- **C173 KDIGO 2022**: confirms the negative. No C-peptide, HOMA, QUICKI, TyG, Matsuda or fasting-insulin term anywhere in about 805,000 characters.
- **C178 PTDM consensus 2024**: confirms. Opinion Statement 1 is an OGTT (glucose); GRADE called flawed; insulin and C-peptide appear only in a study table.
- **C182 Hani 2024**: adds nuance. The WBISI formula is in the text and uses only 0 and 120 minute values; ICC values are not in the stored text.
