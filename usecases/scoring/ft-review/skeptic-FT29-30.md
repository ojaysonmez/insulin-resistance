## 3. Claim-issue verdicts

### C163-A (new, affects FT29 issues 2, 3 and 4 and the reader's table): the HOMA subgroup is from a supplemental analysis with the 2004 stroke definition
In `corpus/fulltext/C163.txt` "HOMA" occurs three times: the abstract/methods (line 117), Table 1 (line 607) and the subgroup block at line 865. That block is immediately followed by the caption "Supplemental Figure 2. Risk of Stroke (2004 criteria) by Treatment Group within Patient Subgroups". Its other rows differ from the main text: prior stroke yes 37/246 vs 26/242, HR 1.40 (0.85-2.31), no 0.70 (0.53-0.91), P 0.02, whereas the main text gives HR 1.23 (0.77-1.95) for prior stroke and 0.65 (0.50-0.84) for no prior stroke. Supplemental Figure 1 (same definition) gives an overall HR 0.82 (0.65-1.03), log-rank P 0.09, against the primary analysis HR 0.75 (0.60-0.94). The main-text Figure 2 legend (line 744) has no extractable data, and no HOMA row exists outside the supplement.
Consequences:
- The numbers 0.95 / 0.69 / P 0.19 (and HbA1c strata P 0.96) are from a sensitivity analysis under the older stroke definition that did not reach significance overall. They cannot be reported as the HOMA subgroup of the primary stroke analysis. The FT29 reader called it "the supplement Figure 2" but did not say that it is the 2004-criteria analysis, and FT29 `claim_issues` 2 and 4 drop the qualifier.
- The main text says "Analyses of subgroups defined by other baseline patient features did not reveal significant modifications of the treatment effect, except for ... history of stroke" (line 253) and later adds index event type P 0.05. If HOMA is in the main Figure 2 (the ledger says its legend "defines HOMA"; I could not confirm this from the stored text), its main-analysis P is below 0.05 only if the text were wrong, so it is above 0.05; the exact value is still not seen.
- Direction: the supplemental point estimates are 0.95 above and 0.69 below the median, so a higher HOMA-IR did not show a larger stroke benefit, if anything the reverse; the interaction is non-significant (P 0.19).
