# FT7 full-text review

## Papers
- C168 (PMID 32867516, text partial): **adds nuance**. The local file holds only the executive-summary pages (to p. 872, 319 lines) and the reference list. Table 2 and the monitoring text are not in it. The text has no occurrence of insulin, HOMA, C-peptide or 'insulin resistance'. Quote: "Not applicable: no passage on insulin or C-peptide testing exists in the local text."
- C187 (PMID 41358885, text good): **confirms**. Full PMC text read. Recommendations use A1C, CGM and glucose; postpartum rec 15.30 is a 75-g OGTT at 4-12 weeks. Insulin resistance is described as physiology (progressive in 2nd/3rd trimester, drops rapidly after delivery). Zero hits for C-peptide, HOMA, antibodies or fasting insulin. Quote: "Insulin resistance drops rapidly with the delivery of the placenta."
- C003 (PMID 12401750, text good): **confirms**. Confirms design and numbers: fasting HOMA-IR measured at 4-day intervals on 10 consecutive occasions in 12 postmenopausal women with diet-controlled T2D and 11 matched controls. The +90%/-47% critical difference comes from 2.77 x CVI on log-transformed data and is given only for the T2D group. Control variation was Gaussian and smaller (mean intraindividual variation 1.05 vs 0.15, P<0.001; the unit is not clear in the extracted text). Quote: "a subsequent sample must increase by 90% or decrease by 47% to be considered significantly different from the first."
- C009 (PMID 17272483, text good): **adds nuance**. Abstract confirmed. Added detail: 12 assays from 9 manufacturers were evaluated but results are for 10; CV was measured on 40 patient samples and was greatest at low insulin concentrations; within-assay CV 3.7-39.0%; 6 of 10 assays met a 32% total-error limit; 7 of 10 recovered added insulin within 15.5%. Cross-reactivity with proinsulin varied. Quote: "A common insulin reference preparation did not change the among-assay CV and failed to improve harmonization of results among assays."
- C012 (PMID 18535197, text partial): **adds nuance**. Text read; tables (Table 2, Fig. 1) are not in the extracted file, so the exact per-assay ranges 0.8-2.0, 1.9-3.2, 1.5-2.9 could not be checked. Text gives pooled medians (IQR): 1.30 (0.84-1.65) normoglycaemia, 2.58 (1.24-3.12) prediabetes, 2.13 (1.19-3.54) T2D, and 'HOMA estimates varied by up to twofold' by assay. Small groups (n=12, 18, 67; total 97). Calculator choice changed HOMA-IR by 15%; 4.3% of insulin results <20 pmol/L and 1% >300 pmol/L were out of calculator limits. Quote: "HOMA estimates varied by up to twofold, depending on which insulin assay was used."
- C045 (PMID 28150502, text good): **contradicts**. The factor 1.8 is confirmed (8.35 uIU/mL Diametra to 14.71 uIU/mL Roche). But the paper does report CVs: intra-assay CV 0.8-10.1% and inter-assay CV 1.0-12.9% from quality-control material. It does not report an among-assay CV. So 'reports no CV' is wrong as written. Quote: "serum insulin measurement with different assays showed a maximum of 1.8-fold difference"
- C048 (PMID 28660493, text good): **confirms**. Confirms 25% (25.0%), ten non-diabetic individuals, seven laboratories; the 25% is driven by insulin (25.4%) not glucose (4.6%). HOMA-IR 2.0 in Helsinki corresponded to 1.3, 1.6, 1.8, 1.8, 2.0, 2.1 elsewhere, derived from regression of liver fat on HOMA-IR per centre (population-level, not one patient), so MEAS-48 holds. Lab means ranged 0.69-4.0 for HOMA-IR. The DOI is 10.1007/s00125-017-4340-1 (now recorded). Main population finding: upper 95th percentile HOMA-IR 1.9 and 2.0 (FINRISK n=1167, FIN-D2D n=459); NAFLD cut-off 1.9 (sensitivity 87%, specificity 79%). Quote: "The inter-laboratory CV% of HOMA-IR was 25% due to inter-assay variation in insulin (25%) rather than glucose (5%)"
- C051 (PMID 29466592, text good): **adds nuance**. Confirms n=609 and P=0.47 (3-way term, beta 0.08 per 10 uIU/mL, 95% CI -0.13 to 0.28). But the insulin hypothesis was added after the trial began (when funding rose) and before examining data; originally an insulin-sensitivity measure was planned. Power: more than 80% for the original n=400, about 90% post hoc for 609. The effect-modifier is the 30-minute insulin of a 75-g OGTT (proxy for insulin secretion), mean 93 uIU/mL. Adults 18-50, BMI 28-40, no diabetes. Quote: "Prior to examining any data, we modified the primary hypothesis of our study and tested baseline INS-30"
- C053 (PMID 29940866, text good): **adds nuance**. Confirms: one-to-one monotonic transforms, so Spearman, ROC and rank-based tests are identical; they are not linear transforms of each other (HOMA-IR and FIRI are linear, QUICKI is not). Simulation, not clinical data. The paper does not discuss within-person CV; the '4.1% reflects its log transform' remark is the project's inference. Quote: "We have shown that HOMA-IR, QUICKI and FIRI are one-to-one transformations of each other."

## Claim issues
- C045 | merged-conclusion.md line 47 (Tohidi 2017 bullet) and claims MEAS-15: 'It reports no CV' is wrong; the paper reports intra-assay (0.8-10.1%) and inter-assay (1.0-12.9%) CVs from QC material. It reports no among-assay CV. Proposed: Tohidi 2017 (PMID 28150502, full text): 8 assays on 165 serum samples, lowest and highest median differing by a factor of 1.8; it reports within-assay CVs (intra-assay 0.8-10.1%, inter-assay 1.0-12.9%) but no among-assay CV.
- C051 | merged-conclusion.md line 76 (DIETFITS table row) and TRI-01, TRI-23: 'prespecified' needs a caveat: the insulin interaction was added after enrolment began, before data were examined; powered at over 80% for n=400, about 90% post hoc for 609. Proposed: DIETFITS (PMID 29466592, full text): n=609; diet x 30-minute post-glucose insulin interaction, specified before data were examined but added after the trial began, P = 0.47 (beta 0.08 per 10 uIU/mL, 95% CI -0.13 to 0.28); non-diabetic adults, weight loss outcome.
- C009 | merged-conclusion.md line 46 (Marcovina bullet) and MEAS-14: Abstract-level label can be upgraded; add that CV depends on concentration and 12 assays were assessed but 10 analysed. Proposed: Marcovina 2007 (PMID 17272483, full text): 10 of 12 assays analysed on 40 patient samples; among-assay CV 12-66%, median 24%, highest at low insulin concentrations; a common reference preparation did not lower it (older-generation assays).
- C012 | merged-conclusion.md line 43 (Manley 2008) and MEAS-10: The per-assay ranges 0.8-2.0 / 1.9-3.2 / 1.5-2.9 could not be verified because the tables are missing from the local text; the text gives pooled medians and 'up to twofold' by assay. Groups are small (12 normoglycaemic). Proposed: HOMA-IR varied up to twofold depending on the insulin assay across 11 assays in 97 fasting adults (Manley 2008, PMID 18535197; 12 normoglycaemic, 18 prediabetic, 67 T2D); per-assay ranges were not re-checked against the table.
- C168 | merged-conclusion.md line 20 and GL-23 (APA 2020): Local full text is the executive summary only; Table 2 and 'risk factor and mechanism' wording not verifiable here. Keep the excerpt label. Proposed: APA 2020 (PMID 32867516): the executive-summary text read names no insulin, HOMA-IR or C-peptide test; Table 2 not re-read.
- C187 | merged-conclusion.md line 21 and GL-24, EV-12 (ADA s15): Read level can change from 'summarising-fetch excerpts' to full text; claim confirmed. The step to non-pregnant HOMA-IR cutoffs is still inference. Proposed: ADA Standards 2026 section 15 (PMID 41358885, full text): GDM follow-up uses 75-g OGTT at 4-12 weeks postpartum and A1C goals; no insulin, HOMA-IR, TyG or C-peptide test is named; insulin resistance rises in the 2nd and 3rd trimester and drops rapidly after delivery.
- C053 | merged-conclusion.md line 42 and MEAS-08: Equivalence holds for rank-based methods; the paper says nothing about within-person CV, so the 'does not make it more informative' sentence is project inference. Proposed: HOMA-IR, QUICKI and FIRI are one-to-one monotone transforms of one another, so rank-based statistics are identical (PMID 29940866, simulation); the interpretation of QUICKI's 4.1% CV as a log-transform effect is the project's inference.
- C048 | merged-conclusion.md line 50 (Isokuortti, 'DOI not recorded in the files') and MEAS-19: DOI is known and in the paper header. Proposed: (Isokuortti 2017, PMID 28660493, DOI 10.1007/s00125-017-4340-1; 25%, ten samples, seven labs)
