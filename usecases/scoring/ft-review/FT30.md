# FT30 full-text review (C208, C219, C316)

All three texts read locally and judged good (C208 lacks Table 1 and the appendix; C316 read by keyword windows).

## Per paper
- **C208 IRIS design (Viscoli 2014)**: confirms. HOMA-IR above 3.0 as entry criterion (highest quartile of non-diabetic populations), screening at least 14 days after the event, insulin by Linco RIA or a converted ELISA. HOMA-IR is among the 13 prespecified subgroup variables. Measure type: fasting-based.
- **C219 IRIS diabetes prevention (Inzucchi 2016)**: adds nuance. Diabetes 3.8% vs 7.7% (HR 0.48). By HOMA-IR median 4.6: HR 0.40 above, 0.66 below, interaction P 0.10; glucose and HbA1c strata behave the same (P 0.11, 0.57). Subgroups not prespecified, unadjusted. HR 0.76 for stroke/MI is quoted correctly.
- **C316 ADA Standards 2026, section 8**: confirms. Rec 8.26 names no insulin or C-peptide test; diagnosis is "primarily" clinical with exclusion of other causes. Zero mentions of C-peptide, mixed meal, OGTT, HOMA.
