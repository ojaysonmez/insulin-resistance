# FT22 full-text review (C238, C240, C244, C258, C261, C262, C263)

Text quality: good for all except C258 (partial: PDF with interleaved columns, Methods and Table 4 readable). No web access used; about 22 tool calls.

## Per paper
- **C238 Mitchell 2019 (row 50 D8): adds nuance.** 798 adults on antipsychotics, one Belgian centre. Fasting insulin (AUC 0.72) and HOMA-IR (0.80) were less accurate than fasting glucose (0.935) and 2-h OGTT (0.947) against glucose-based definitions (which contain those tests). Recommended algorithm is HbA1c then conventional tests. Incremental value of insulin was not tested. Measure: fasting-based.
- **C240 Lara 2019 (row 27 D7): confirms.** 830 TPIAT (825 for chronic pancreatitis) in 2002 to 2013, weighted national estimates, adults, neoplasia excluded: about 70 a year on average, a 12-year total. No current volume and no C-peptide data.
- **C244 McCoy 2020 (row 3 D7): confirms.** Sulfonylurea without insulin: 5.75 (5.08 to 6.42) hypoglycaemia ED visits or admissions per 1,000 person-years (49,200 of 201,705 insured adults). Claims-based lower bound; silent on whether insulin, C-peptide or drug screen was sent.
- **C258 Kakko 2022 (row 50 D2, D8): contradicts the reading.** The HOMA-IR cut point of 1.98 (sensitivity and specificity 83%) was derived against a label defined by HOMA-IR percentile (>=75th), so it is circular, not diagnostic accuracy. n = 55, children, uncontrolled, fasting-based.
- **C261 Winter 2022 (no score entry; row 6 background): adds nuance.** Review: C-peptide, insulin and proinsulin separate type 1 from type 2 diabetes only at the extremes; autoantibodies establish type 1. C-peptide <0.6 ng/mL is only "suggested" by a cited source. No claim in the project documents cites it.
- **C262 Ward 2022 (row 27 D4): adds nuance.** Survey of 24 pancreas and 18 islet allotransplant programs (not TPIAT). Fasting C-peptide routine (92% and 94%); MMTT recommended annually by 72% of islet programs; no objective cutoffs; no assay data. The "not standardized" statement is background from 2015, not a survey result.
- **C263 Sims 2022 (row 49 D7): confirms.** TrialNet >250,000 relatives (text says over 220,000), >=2 autoantibodies 2.5%. No insulin or C-peptide test named in staging or follow-up; "standards for metabolic follow-up have not been established".

## Proposals
- Row 50 D8: 1 to 0 (low confidence; C238 and C258). Insulin did worse than glucose tests in the one large study, and the only child study cannot show added value. Keeping 1 is defensible.
- Row 50 D2, row 27 D7, row 3 D7, row 27 D4, row 49 D7: no change (null).
