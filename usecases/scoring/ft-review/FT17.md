# FT17 full-text review (rows 32 and 47)

All six texts are good quality. Scores read from V1 to V5; no later correction found for the cited entries.

## Per paper
- C230 Benoit 2018 (MMWR, row 32 D7): adds nuance. 188,950 DKA hospitalizations in 2014, all ages, no diabetes type. No insulin or C-peptide content.
- C246 Benoit 2020 (row 32 D7): confirms. Table 1 matches the V1 numbers (184,255 adult events; 168,185 inpatient; 16,070 ED-only; 70.6% type 1; 53,941 type 2). Coding unvalidated, events not patients, no new-onset split; the 20% new-onset and 35% ketosis-prone shares are not from this paper.
- C063 Buzzetti 2020 (row 47 D7): adds nuance. LADA is 2-12% of all patients with diabetes (lower in primary care, African American and Hispanic people). It also recommends C-peptide in autoantibody-positive patients (tiers <0.3, 0.3-0.7, >0.7 nmol/L; fasting, random or postprandial; repeat at treatment failure). This bears on row 47 D3.
- C104 Shimada 2024 (row 47, many dimensions): confirms. Fasting serum C-peptide <0.6 ng/mL is criterion 3 for definite SPIDDM; no derivation, no assay, no glucose or timing condition; treatment excluded from the criteria.
- C285 Kawasaki 2024 (row 47 D2, D8): adds nuance. n=60, 30 progressed (7 already <0.6 at entry). Fasting C-peptide <1.41 ng/mL raised PPV only to 81.0% (RIA) and 79.0% (ELISA) from 70.0% and 73.1%, like age or BMI. Same registry as C306.
- C306 Noso 2025 (row 47 D2): adds nuance. The 9.1% is undetectable C-peptide; F-CPR <0.6 ng/mL was reached by 41.3% at 5 y and 57.9% at 10 y (n=105 slowly progressive). Fasting C-peptide is the outcome.

## Proposals
- Row 47 D3: 1 to 3 (low confidence; fallback 2), from C063. International expert consensus recommends C-peptide in autoantibody-positive patients and repeats it to identify progression. It is a panel statement (says there are no guidelines), endorsement not verified, thresholds in nmol/L.
- No change (null): row 47 D2 (2, 1 arguable), D8 (2), D1 (3), D7 (3); row 32 D7 (3).

## Claim issues
- V1 row 47 D7: "2-12% of adult-onset diabetes" should read all patients with diabetes; African American and Hispanic rates are lower.
- usecases-verified.md row 47: add the C063 link (international consensus recommending C-peptide in antibody-positive patients; thresholds differ from 0.6 ng/mL).
- V2 row 47 D2: replace the 9.1% undetectable figure with 41.3% (5 y) and 57.9% (10 y) for <0.6 ng/mL; C285 and C306 overlap.
- V2 row 47 D8: give the fasting C-peptide step specifically (70.0 to 81.0% RIA; 73.1 to 79.0% ELISA).

Path to viability, row 47: affected (C063 offers an international consensus route; cutoffs differ from the Japanese criterion). Row 32: not affected.
