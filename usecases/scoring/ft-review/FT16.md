## Per paper
- **C204 Peranteau 2013 (PMID 24314196, DOI 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2013.04.022), row 24 D2: confirms.** Eight patients aged 4 to 26, one US centre, surgical localisation focus. Insulin at hypoglycaemia is an inclusion criterion only; no test accuracy or decision-impact data.
- **C321 Mitteer 2026 (PMID 42149805, DOI 10.1159/000552145), row 24 D7: adds nuance.** The site is Philadelphia (US), 23 patients aged 2 to 26 over 2006 to 2024. Critical-sample insulin (AUC 0.61) and C-peptide (AUC 0.505) did not separate insulinoma from congenital HI; BOHB (AUC 0.75) did. Supports the low commonality bin but does not settle it.
- **C029 Funakoshi 2011 (PMID 24843502, DOI 10.1111/j.2040-1124.2010.00096.x), row 31 D2, D8, S, D1: adds nuance.** It is about who needs insulin to reach target in hospitalised Japanese type 2 patients (fasting and glucagon-stimulated C-peptide, AUC 0.69 to 0.75, LR 1.6 to 2.8), not about stopping insulin or classifying doubtful type; not random C-peptide.
- **C226 Rogers 2017 (PMID 29115947, DOI 10.1186/s12916-017-0958-6), row 31 D7: adds nuance.** 37,000 incident adult type 1 cases per year is a claims-algorithm extrapolation, not a count of doubtful-type adults; an incidence cross-check only.
- **C278 Lancet Reg Health Eur 2023 (PMID 37273894, DOI 10.1016/j.lanepe.2023.100661), row 31 D7: adds nuance.** A short comment with no data; "up to 40%" refers to prevalent adults over 30 with type 1 who might have been misdiagnosed.
- **C004 Maldonado 2003 (PMID 14602731, DOI 10.1210/jc.2003-030180), row 32 D2, D8: confirms.** n=103 prospective cohort; C-peptide (fasting and 1 mg glucagon) after correction of DKA; groups partly defined by the same cut-offs.
- **C008 Balasubramanyam 2006 (PMID 17130187, DOI 10.2337/dc06-0749), row 32 D2, D8, D4, D1, D6, S: adds nuance.** Comparator AUCs now read: 0.972 against 0.703 to 0.766 for the other schemes, but the outcome is the same glucagon C-peptide test at 12 months (circular), and baseline testing was within 1 to 2 weeks of DKA.
- **C011 Balasubramanyam 2008 (PMID 18292467, DOI 10.1210/er.2007-0026), row 32 D2: adds nuance.** Review by the same group: test 1 to 3 weeks after resolution; the cut-offs "do not independently predict" safe insulin withdrawal; the fasting C-peptide to glucose ratio above 11 does so among beta-positive patients.

## Claim issues
1. usecases-verified.md row 32 caveat "not within ~2 weeks of the acute episode" (and the V3 "weeks to months") is not supported: the cohorts tested after correction of DKA, within 1 to 2 weeks; the review says 1 to 3 weeks after resolution.
2. Row 32 "can insulin be withdrawn" overstates what the n=294 cohort shows (it predicts C-peptide-defined beta-cell function at 12 months).
3. Row 31 "Likelihood ratios ~2" for Funakoshi: 1.6 to 2.8, fasting or glucagon-stimulated, outcome is needing insulin to reach target.
4. V8 row 24 D7: the cohort is at a US centre and aged 2 to 26.
5. V1 row 31 D7: the 40% in C278 is "up to", prevalent adults over 30, no data.
