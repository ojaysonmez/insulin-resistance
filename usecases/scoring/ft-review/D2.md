# D2 discovery scan (digest-2)

Scope: 112 entries read from scratch/discovery/digest-2.txt, mostly abstract-level. Strengths are abstract-level and the digest rows are the only evidence read. Uses not covered by rows 1 to 50. One grep was run on local full texts.

## Candidates, ranked by promise (none looks better than tier C or D)

1. **Data-driven type 2 diabetes subtypes** (C101, C269). HOMA2-B and HOMA2-IR (fasting-based) in new type 2 diabetes to choose therapy. Consensus report C101 says clustering was replicated in 22 studies but individual-level probabilities are often low. Strength weak. Nearest row 17. Likely tier D. Needs a trial of subtype-guided treatment.
2. **Risk-stratified lifestyle intervention in prediabetes** (C073, PLIS RCT, 1105 people). Post-load OGTT insulin secretion and sensitivity plus liver fat define a high-risk phenotype. Strength moderate for the design, result for the decision not seen in the excerpt. Nearest row 17 and 45. Likely tier D, possible C if replicated.
3. **Pre-transplant C-peptide for pancreas-kidney transplant selection** (C171, C166). Retrospective, conflicting direction. Strength weak. Nearest row 10 (which is post-transplant classification). Likely tier D.
4. **OGTT insulin and C-peptide ratios to diagnose insulinoma without the 72-h fast** (C249; C245 context). One case-control, 37 vs 42, AUC 0.97, no validation. Strength weak. Nearest row 23. Likely tier D. Needs prospective consecutive-patient validation against the fast.
5. **IR index for NASH or fibrosis triage in severe obesity and NAFLD follow-up** (C243, C075, C254). HOMA-IR and OGIS (post-load); small single-centre associations. Strength weak. Nearest row 14. Likely tier D.
6. **Metformin for bipolar depression with insulin resistance** (C079, one RCT, result not in excerpt). Strength weak. Nearest row 13. Likely tier D.
7. **Baseline insulin or HOMA as oncology biomarker** (C170 MA.32 analysis, C165 mouse data). Strength weak, mostly preclinical. Nearest row 40. Research-only.
8. **Estimated insulin sensitivity (equation, non-insulin surrogate) in youth type classification** (C252). Strength weak. Nearest row 33. Likely tier D.
9. **HOMA-IR criterion in the 2020 MAFLD definition for lean adults** (C061). Not confirmed: no 'HOMA' hit in the local text. Unverified, weak. Nearest row 14. Superseded by MASLD (C097).

Guideline or regulator mention found for any of these: none confirmed in the corpus (C101 is a consensus report that discusses subtypes as a gap).
