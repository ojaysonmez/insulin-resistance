# FT1 full-text review (rows 8, 9)

Texts: C013, C060, C049, C116, C136 good; C005 partial (two-column PDF text). Current scores are in the FT1.json notes.

## Per paper
- C005 Palmer 2004: confirms. Consensus that stimulated C-peptide is the trial outcome; DCCT threshold 0.2 nmol/l; test between 70 and 200 mg/dl. Trial endpoint, not a care test.
- C013 Greenbaum 2008: adds nuance. MMTT beats glucagon test (nausea 75-81% with glucagon), but fasting C-peptide correlates r=0.95/0.93 with MMTT AUC and the authors say a stimulated test may add nothing in practice.
- C060 Albiglutide 2020: confirms endpoint use. Negative trial (-0.13 vs -0.16 nmol/L, P=0.65), n=67, stopped early, GSK-funded.
- C049 Woodmansey 2017: adds nuance. No C-peptide in the paper; supports misclassification (87.8% as type 2) and insulin need only.
- C116 Juza 2025: adds nuance. Fasting C-peptide 0.81 vs 1.71 nmol/L (n=44 vs 35); the cut-off table has insulin (<85 pmol/L, sens 75.0, spec 82.9), not C-peptide; low power.
- C136 Alavi 2026: confirms anecdotal. Single C-peptide 0.87 ng/mL; insulin drawn on exogenous insulin.
