## Per paper
- **C006 (ADA/APA/AACE/NAASO 2004, row 50 D1 and S): confirms.** Table 3 monitors history, BMI, waist, blood pressure, fasting plasma glucose, fasting lipids. No insulin, C-peptide or HOMA test. Insulin only as mechanism and research text. Row 50 D1 2 and S 1 stand. Claim issue: row 43 says "not read at source"; now read.
- **C027 (PancreasFest 2012, rows 9 and 27): adds nuance.** Classification uses islet antibodies and pancreatic polypeptide, not C-peptide. Statement 2.4 (TPIAT reserve) has the weakest agreement (A 45%, D 18%, E 8%), and its supporting text does name serum C-peptide during an OGTT or mixed-nutrient meal. Arginine testing is "not routinely available". Research recommendations say outcome value is unknown.
- **C030 (Imagawa 2012, row 46): confirms.** C-peptide is criterion 3 of 3; the cut-offs were not revised and "data not shown", so no accuracy data (D2 1 stands). Serum criterion needs fasting below 0.3 AND stimulated below 0.5 ng/mL. The 2004 screening criteria use no C-peptide (supports S 1). Authors' "used worldwide" is not evidence of US adoption (D3 1 stands).
- **C036 (Rudland 2015, row 35 D7): not relevant to insulin.** Glucose-based GCK branch only: 4 of 776 GDM women, criteria fasting glucose 5.5 mmol/L or more plus BMI below 25 kg/m2. No D7 change.
- **C037 (Insel 2015, row 49): adds nuance.** Explicitly "not recommended at this time for clinical practice" (2015). Stimulated C-peptide falls about 6 months before diagnosis, fasting C-peptide stays normal; 20% C-peptide fall: 47% 4-year risk, PPV 78%. Fasting insulin secretion and total postglucose insulin output were not predictive. Scores D3 1, D2 2, D1 1 stand.
- **C040 (Alptekin 2016, row 35 part b): adds nuance.** 227 analysed of 250 enrolled, 20 GDM. HOMA-IR AUC 0.809 at in-sample cut-off 2.08 (sens 90, spec 61, NPV 98, PPV 18); BMI AUC 0.723. No validation, no combined model, no glucose comparison. Printed HOMA-IR formula looks odd (mg/L, 0.05), possibly typesetting. D2 2, D8 1 stand.
- **C041 (IRIS, row 13): confirms.** HOMA-IR above 3.0 only as entry (median 4.7), central laboratories, no HOMA-IR-stratified result in the main text, HR 0.76, 9.0 vs 11.8 percent. Prediabetes was not an entry criterion. Heart failure did not differ (74 vs 71). All row 13 scores stand.
