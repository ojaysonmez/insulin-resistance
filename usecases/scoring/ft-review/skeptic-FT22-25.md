## 2. Score-proposal verdict

| Row, dim | Current (entry) | Proposed | Verdict |

| 50 D8 | 1 (V26, low confidence) | 0 (FT22, low confidence, C238 and C258) | REJECT. Keep 1. |

Reasons, each checked:
1. Anchor 0 is "alternatives do as well or better". The evidence offered does not show that in the stated population (youth on second-generation antipsychotics, measure type fasting insulin). C238 is adults (798, mean age 37.7), both first- and second-generation drugs (text: "receiving first-generation drugs", 41.1% treated for more than one year), single Belgian centre. Its Table 4 compares single tests against the "old definition of diabetes", and the Results say that definition "incorporat[es] IFG and OGTT" (found verbatim). So fasting glucose (AUC 0.935) and the 2-h OGTT (0.947) are in the reference; their lead over fasting insulin (0.722) and HOMA-IR (0.800) is partly built in. FT22 states this itself ("incorporation"). It cannot be read as "the alternatives do as well or better" for incremental value.
2. The study did not test whether insulin adds to the glucose tests (FT22 says so; C238's algorithm text contains no insulin step: "step 1. HBA1c >=5.7%; if negative, test again in 6 months, but if step 1 is positive, proceed to conventional testing (HBA1c and fasting glucose and OGTT ...)").
3. C258 is confirmed circular (see claim issue 1) and so supports neither 1 nor 0. FT22 itself says it "cannot show added value". Removing a weak positive does not create a negative.
4. Clarification 19 allows "not determinable" and says mechanism-only evidence scores at most 2; it does not say that a missing incremental-value study scores 0. Row 50 D8 = 1 already carries "No study showing that fasting insulin changes the answer ... was found" and confidence low; that is the correct position.
5. Effect size: 1 to 0 lowers UQ for row 50 by one third of a point on D8 on the strength of a single adult study with an incorporated reference. The FT22 reader's own caveat ("keeping 1 is defensible") is the better call.
What does change: the reason text of V26 row 50 D8 should be corrected (claim issues 1 and 2) so that it names the C238 comparison as evidence of weaker single-test performance, with the incorporation caveat.

## 4. "No change" calls: where the reader may be wrong

Row 11 D7 arithmetic from C289 (about 31.9%): partly wrong, no consequence for the bin.
- 109,476 / 343,727 = 31.85% mixes the numerator (sum over six procedure columns) with the count of unique individuals; the column totals add to 349,411, so on a common base the share is 31.3%. Report "about 31 to 32 percent (31.3% on the table's own column totals; 31.9% against the 343,727 unique individuals)".
- Check of the chain: V20 used 28 to 37% with a 200,000 point value. Using C284's 2022 value (280,000; counts procedures, includes about 11% revisions and endoscopic procedures) times 0.31 = about 87,000; V20's 2024 value (200,667, web excerpt, not in C284) times 0.31 = about 62,000. Both are bin 3 (10,000 to 100,000). Hold at 3.
- Note that C289's percentages come from ICD codes in the 6 months before the index procedure (found: "baseline characteristics ... in the 6-months before the index procedure were identified using ICD-9-CM and ICD-10 codes"), commercial claims only (MarketScan), with at least 180 days of continuous enrolment; Medicare and Medicaid patients are not in it. The FT23 reader's caveat is right.
- The share is diabetes of any kind and the C-peptide use is limited to insulin-untreated patients with T2D; V20 already says the useful subgroup is smaller.

Row 45 D3 anchor question (V20 scored 2; the literal "not addressed" anchor gives 1): ACCEPT the no-change call, with the conflict recorded for the user. The text shows (C317) that Rec 3.7 recommends metformin for "high-risk individuals" including "individuals with prior gestational diabetes mellitus" (found), and the section has no insulin, C-peptide or HOMA measure for GDM (the only HOMA is in the IRIS description of Rec 3.13). Clarification 5 reads: "A guideline that recommends a drug or names the condition the test identifies but not the test scores 2". Rec 3.7 recommends a drug; so the rule gives 2, and V20 followed it explicitly (and rated its own choice medium, noting the anchor conflict). Two cautions: (1) the same rule gave row 11 D3 = 2 (ASMBS/IFSO recommends surgery, no C-peptide mention in the text searched), so a change for row 45 alone would make the rows inconsistent; (2) clarification 5 is arguably meant for the case where the recommended drug is conditioned on the condition the test identifies (row 13: "evidence of insulin resistance and prediabetes"), which is not true for Rec 3.7 (metformin for prior GDM regardless of insulin resistance). If the user reads it that way, rows 45 and 11 (and any other rule-5 rows with a drug but no condition link) would move to 1 together. That is a rubric decision for the user, not a full-text finding; I recommend recording it in DECISIONS.md and not changing row 45 alone. Also note that FT24's reader counts row 13 correctly at 2: Rec 3.13 conditions pioglitazone on "evidence of insulin resistance" (condition named, test not), and the HOMA-IR 3.0 is in the IRIS description only.

Row 49 D7 (C263 and C308): no-change calls agreed. C263's ">250,000" versus "over 220,000" inconsistency is real. The D7 bin rests on assumptions, as the V1 reason already says.

Row 22 D8 (C322): no-change (keep 2) agreed, for a stronger reason than the reader gives. The "defined subgroup" anchor (2) is where D8 sits when sampling is useful only in imaging-negative patients; C322 shows the subgroup shrinks (72% of the imaging-negative patients had a positive PET) but not that the remaining 16 (28%) are better served by sampling, nor that PET replaces it. Also, the 72-h fasting test in 80% and Whipple's triad define the cohort, so C322 gives no support or challenge to the insulin gradient itself. The FT25 `path_to_viability` statement "the missing piece stays valid" is right.

Row 9 D7 (C328): no-change agreed (scored population is TPIAT evaluations; the 30% figure is only in the unscored note). The note should carry "cumulative, adults with chronic pancreatitis", as in section 3.

Other no-change calls (rows 3 D7, 27 D4, 27 D7, 14 D7, 14 D8, 46 D2, 13 D3, 8 D4, 21 D2 and D4, 33 D7): agreed on the quotes and numbers I re-checked.
