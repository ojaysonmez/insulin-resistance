# FT20 full-text review (C096, C103, C108, C109, C110, C118, C119)

No score change is proposed. Ten claim issues are listed, one of them a citation that does not support its row (C119, row 27). Details in FT20.json.

## Per paper
- **C096** (PMID 36727674, text partial): confirms. The local text is body text only (about 70,000 characters, 174 lines; Tables 1 to 3 and the reference list are not in the extraction). In that text there is no mention of HOMA-IR, fasting insulin, C-peptide, OGTT, Matsuda or QUICKI. Insulin resistance appears as pathogenesis, and the only glycaemia test advice is screening NAFLD patients for T2DM, with physical examination for 'signs of insulin resistance'. It is a Guidance, not a GRADE guideline. Quote: "patients with NAFLD should be screened for the presence of T2DM (Table 1)"
- **C103** (PMID 38017369, text good): adds nuance. Prospective cohort in Qazvin, Iran (583 women, GDM 15.4%), fasting HOMA-IR at 14 weeks or less, GDM by 75 g OGTT at 24 to 28 weeks. Overall AUC was only 0.642 (cut-off 2.71, sensitivity 61.1%, specificity 63.9%); AUC 0.638 (cut-off 2.06) for BMI under 25 and 0.622 (cut-off 3.13) for BMI 25 or more; in women 35 years or older HOMA-IR did not differ (P=0.873). Nothing tests added value over age, GDM history or glucose, and cut-offs are in-sample. Quote: "In women ≥ 35 years old, there is no yield of HOMA-IR for GDM prediction."
- **C108** (PMID 38851997, text partial): adds nuance. Wording confirmed in this copy (PDF text extraction, two columns, garbled layout but the passages are readable). The level of evidence and grade (LoE 3, weak recommendation, consensus) are visible here, unlike in the PMC copy described in the row. Table 7 sits under a separate LoE 2 strong recommendation and lists fasting insulin and/or C-peptide and HOMA-IR in the main list for 'Type 2 diabetes or insulin resistance'; OGTT or mixed-meal indices are under 'Further investigations*' (according to clinical evaluation and a priori probability). The text says HOMA-IR can use fasting insulin or C-peptide (not on insulin treatment) and that interpretation needs standardised insulin or C-peptide measurement; no management step follows from the result. Quote: "may be considered to clarify metabolic dysfunction in adults with (suspected) MASLD and without an established diagnosis of type 2 diabetes"

This executive summary carries the same recommendation and Table 7. - **C118** (PMID 39992257, text good): confirms. TrialNet Milan cohort (n=57: 30 Stage 1, 27 Stage 2), 5-point OGTT with glucose, insulin and C-peptide. Progression in 16 of 57 over a median 19 +/- 15 months. In a logistic model with 7 covariates, only oral-minimal-model phi total was significant (OR 0.922, 0.863-0.985, P=.016); AUC C-peptide was not (0.999, P=.330). DPTRS and Index60 were higher in Stage 2 (14 of 16 with DPTRS above 7.00 were Stage 2) and in progressors, but their prognostic accuracy was not tested here. Stated limits: short follow-up, small size, no healthy controls, no 10 and 20 minute samples. The paper is mainly a trial-endpoint (research) study. Quote: "Other variables, including age, BMI, sex, AUC C-peptide, OMM-derived insulin sensitivity, and stage, did not show significant associations with progression risk."
- **C119** (PMID 39994886, text good): not relevant. It is a laminin alpha 5 (LAMA5) tissue and plasma biomarker study in 29 TPIAT patients. Pretransplant 'glucose-stimulated C-peptide' is only listed among recorded assessments; no stimulated C-peptide result, no MMTT and no C-peptide correlation with outcomes is reported. Table 1 gives basal (fasting) C-peptide (1.0 to 2.8 ng/mL by aetiology) and a basal SUITO index. Outcomes correlated with LAMA5 were fasting glucose, HbA1c and islet yield (IEQ), not C-peptide. Quote: "Pretransplant assessments including age, gender, BMI, duration of symptoms, fasting blood glucose, hemoglobin A1c (HbA1c), glucose-stimulated C-peptide"

## Claim issues
- usecases-verified.md row 14 (and claims GL-29, UC-14): The text says the Table 7 wording 'according to clinical evaluation and [a priori] probability' attaches to lipoprotein(a) and introduces the insulin tests. In the full text the asterisk marks only the 'Further investigations*' lines in each block. For type 2 diabetes or insulin resistance, fasting insulin and/or C-peptide and HOMA-IR are in the main list (no asterisk); only 'Insulin resistance indices from oral glucose tolerance test or mixed meal tests' is under 'Further investigations*'. Lipoprotein(a) is in the main list ('Once in a lifetime'). Proposed: Table 7 (attached to a 100%-agreement, LoE 2 strong recommendation that comorbidity tests are recommended at diagnosis and follow-up) lists, for type 2 diabetes or insulin resistance, fasting glucose, HbA1c, OGTT 2-h glucose, fasting insulin and/or C-peptide and HOMA-IR; OGTT- or mixed-meal-derived insulin-resistance indices are listed as 'Further investigations*' (*according to clinical evaluation and a priori probability).
- usecases-verified.md row 14 caveat 'level of evidence not visible in PMC'; claim GL-27: Level of evidence and grade are visible in the stored copies of all three EASL documents (C108 PDF text, C109 and C110 XML text): LoE 3, weak recommendation, consensus; 92% agreement is in C109. Proposed: LoE 3, weak recommendation, consensus, 92% agreement (read in the full text of PMIDs 38851997, 38852583 and 38869512).
- usecases-verified.md row 14 status Limited and the Table 7 tension: Two statements in one guideline: a strong (LoE 2, 100% agreement) recommendation that Table 7 tests, which include fasting insulin and/or C-peptide and HOMA-IR, are recommended at diagnosis; and a weak 'may be considered' for HOMA-IR or OGTT-derived estimates. Row 14 reports only the weak one. Limited stays right for the specific population, but the row should say the table sits under a strong recommendation. Proposed: The guideline lists fasting insulin and/or C-peptide and HOMA-IR in Table 7 under a strong recommendation to test for comorbidities, and separately says assessment of insulin resistance 'may be considered' (weak) to clarify metabolic dysfunction; neither is linked to a management step.
- usecases-verified.md row 14 / claim GL-44 (AASLD negative): The local AASLD file is body text of about 70,000 characters without tables or references; it confirms the negative only for that text. The skeptic's whole-article search is a separate source and was not repeated. Proposed: No HOMA-IR, fasting insulin or C-peptide recommendation was found in the AASLD 2023 body text read here (tables not in the extraction); the earlier whole-article search also found none.
- usecases-verified.md row 35, claims UC-35 and EV-45 ('moderate in-sample discrimination'; 'AUCs of 0.62-0.81'; 'measured at 6-14 weeks'): For PMID 38017369 the full text gives AUC 0.642 overall (0.622 to 0.680 in subgroups), a null result in women 35 or older, and an inclusion rule of gestational age 14 weeks or less (no lower bound, so '6-14 weeks' is not stated for this study). The authors call it a moderate predictor, but the AUCs are low. Proposed: PMID 38017369 (full text): fasting HOMA-IR at 14 weeks or less, AUC 0.642 overall (0.622 to 0.680 in subgroups), in-sample cut-offs 2.06 to 3.13 by BMI, no discrimination in women 35 or older (P=0.873), no test of added value over age, history or BMI.
- V15 D2 reason for row 35: The V15 reason lists AUC 0.73 and 0.81 and cut-offs 1.43 to 2.27, 2.08 and 1.52; none of these belong to PMID 38017369, which has AUC 0.642 and cut-offs 2.71, 2.69, 2.06 and 3.13. The reason should say which paper gives which number. Proposed: Add: PMID 38017369 (full text) AUC 0.642, cut-offs 2.06 (BMI<25) and 3.13 (BMI>=25).
- usecases-verified.md row 49 caveat 'Progressors had lower AUC C-peptide in a univariate comparison (Table S1, not read)': The main text says progressors had lower insulin secretion metrics, including phi total and AUC C-peptide, but gives no numbers or P values; Table S1 is not in the stored text, so significance of the AUC C-peptide difference is still unverified. Proposed: The text says progressors had lower phi total and AUC C-peptide (Table S1 not in the stored text; no values or P values seen); AUC C-peptide did not differ between Stage 1 and Stage 2 (P=.362).
- usecases-verified.md row 49 caveat on the '8-fold reduction per 10 units' text: Confirmed that the discussion says '8 times lower odds' while Table 3 shows OR 0.922 (0.863-0.985). The row's check (0.92 to the 10th power, about 2.3-fold) assumes the OR is per 1 unit, which the paper does not state. Proposed: The text's '8-fold lower odds per 10 units of phi total' does not match its Table 3 OR of 0.922 (95% CI 0.863-0.985); the unit of the OR is not stated, so the effect size is not quoted.
- usecases-verified.md row 49 evidence list (Galderisi 2025): Confirmed: n=30 Stage 1 and 27 Stage 2, phi total the only significant variable. Not stated in the row: median follow-up 19 +/- 15 months, 16 progressors (5 from Stage 1, 11 from Stage 2) against 7 covariates, and DPTRS and Index60 were not in the model. Proposed: Galderisi 2025: 16 progressors (5 of 30 Stage 1, 11 of 27 Stage 2) over a median 19 +/- 15 months; 7-covariate model without DPTRS or Index60.
- usecases-verified.md row 27 source list and claim UC-27 (PMID 39994886 as support for MMTT C-peptide): The full text of PMID 39994886 is a LAMA5 biomarker study. A 'glucose-stimulated C-peptide' is named among recorded pretransplant assessments, but no stimulated or MMTT C-peptide result or correlation is reported; Table 1 shows only basal C-peptide and a basal SUITO index. It does not support MMTT C-peptide before TPIAT. Proposed: PMID 39994886 (full text): pretransplant glucose-stimulated C-peptide was recorded but not analysed; only basal C-peptide is reported; the paper's markers were LAMA5 and tissue matrix proteins. Not a source for MMTT C-peptide.
