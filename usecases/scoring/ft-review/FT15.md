# FT15 full-text review (rows 10, 4, 24, 27, 31, 32)

Reader: full-text reader. All eight papers had local text; seven are good, C098 is partial (tables missing). Details are in `usecases/scoring/ft-review/FT15.json`.

## Per paper
- **C052 Igls 2018 (consensus):** confirms. C-peptide "should be measured before transplantation"; optimal and good grades need a rise over baseline (>0.5 ng/mL, 0.17 nmol/L). The authors call the scheme a work in progress to be validated. No decision-impact evidence.
- **C068 Igls symposium 2021:** adds nuance. CGM gradient confirmed (TIR 58, 75, 90, 100% from failure to optimal, n=55). The authors say the C-peptide and insulin criteria have a weaker rationale than the glycaemic ones, the stimulated test was felt too cumbersome, and it is not known whether Igls predicts outcomes or guides decisions. A preliminary Igls 2.0 is proposed (success from glucose metrics alone; C-peptide only grades the graft).
- **C253 Auto-Igls (n=379):** adds nuance. Tracks islet mass, CGM and IVGTT response. 23% (57/248) had fasting C-peptide <=0.5 ng/mL but stimulated >0.5, and 2 of 10 "failed" patients were insulin independent with normal HbA1c.
- **C271 CITR (n=677):** adds nuance. AUC 0.906 and 0.845 (cut 0.33 nmol/L) confirmed, but concurrent, single-predictor, no comparison with HbA1c, insulin dose or CGM; sites used different, unstandardised tests.
- **C302 OPTN/SRTR 2023:** confirms 915 pancreas transplants (23 paediatric). No islet data. US graft failure is defined by insulin >=0.5 U/kg/day for 90 days, not C-peptide.
- **C111 paediatric TPIAT (n=10):** confirms that no pre-operative C-peptide use is shown; no Igls grading; no support for row 10 S.
- **C224 C-peptide reference system:** adds nuance. Harmonisation was incomplete in 2017 (no serum certified reference material listed; two reference procedures with a bias discrepancy). The "higher concentration" claim is not in the local text.
- **C098 HI guideline 2023:** adds nuance. Rec 1.1 Grade 1 moderate: high-sensitivity insulin at hypoglycaemia, with insulin-action markers taking priority if they disagree. Rec 1.4 Grade 1 low names no insulin or C-peptide test. C-peptide is absent from the local text.
