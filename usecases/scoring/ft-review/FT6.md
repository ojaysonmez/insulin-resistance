# FT6 full-text review (9 papers, all text good)

Verdicts and what changed:
- C142 Xu 2026 (PMID 42702103): adds nuance. C-statistic numbers confirmed (0.792 base, 0.793 TyG, 0.792 HOMA-IR). The lower HOMA-IR hazard appears in tertile Fine-Gray analyses; the authors attribute it to competing non-CVD death, not "reverse causation", and say it is not protective.
- C145 Goodwin 2002 (11773152): confirms. 512 women, top v bottom quartile HR 2.0 recurrence, 3.1 death. Same cohort as C152.
- C146 IADPSG 2010 (20190296): adds nuance. Glucose-only maternal diagnosis confirmed; cord C-peptide is an outcome used to set thresholds, not a test.
- C148 Duggan 2011 (21115858): adds nuance. HOMA HR 1.12 for breast-cancer death; all-cause association lost on log transform and by quartile; 62 deaths; positive in ER-positive disease (unlike 39808164).
- C149 Hung 2011 (21441124): adds nuance. n=12; HOMA-IR r -0.58, "reasonable alternative".
- C152 Goodwin 2012 (22162568): adds nuance. First-5-years pattern confirmed (HR 2.32, 2.85); not significant when insulin and BMI are entered together (P .07, .09).
- C156 Jia 2014 (24476695): adds nuance. 1,074 men (495 CKD); kappa above 0.6 for "the vast majority"; mortality AUC 0.56-0.58 including the clamp; Baxter affiliation.
- C159 King-Morris 2016 (26526047): adds nuance. 10 new PD patients (haemodialysis group only a comparison); HOMA-IR r -0.35; Baxter-supported in part.
- C162 Tsujimoto 2017 (28390156): adds nuance. NHANES all-cancer mortality, effect in non-obese only (HR 1.89), HR varied by assay, 144 deaths; not breast cancer.

Claim issues (exact wording in FT6.json): NHANES bullet (EV-26); line 35 cancer sentence (EV-33; cohorts not independent); line 37 CKD sentence (MEAS-05; 1,074 men); line 37 dialysis clause (MEAS-06; the overlap statement is inexact); line 21 pregnancy bullet (GL-24; now full text).

No score proposals. Confidence: full text leaves confidence unchanged for C142, C145, C148, C152, C156; it lowers confidence in the independence of the cancer evidence (C145 and C152 share a cohort, C162 is not breast cancer).
