# FT32 full-text review (C276, C282, C325)

## Papers
- **C276** Galvez 2023 (PMID 36965853, DOI 10.1016/j.ando.2023.03.015), full text, good (short article). Verdict: confirms row 2 D4. Narrative review plus one case; Elecsys misses most analogues, two assays advised, no guideline. Quote: "no single routinely used method is capable of detecting every insulin analog".
- **C282** Kovacs 2024 (PMID 37888894, DOI 10.1002/dmrr.3743), full text, good. Verdict: adds nuance for row 32 D7. The 35% (24-49%) is from 10 studies and 1,054 patients; 11 studies and 2,010 patients give 25% (14-42%) for DKA at any time. Mostly Asian and African studies, children included, I2 94-97%.
- **C325** Kandaswamy 2026 (PMID 42276696, DOI 10.1016/j.ajt.2026.05.720), full text, good. Verdict: adds nuance for row 10 D7. 821 adult transplants in 2024 (892 in 2023). No C-peptide in the report; graft failure is defined by insulin dose (0.5 units/kg/day for 90 days), so the C-peptide share in the row 10 chain stays an assumption.

## Proposals
