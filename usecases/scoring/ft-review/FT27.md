# FT27 full-text review

Texts: all seven good except C066 (PDF text, two columns interleaved, partial). Only C054 (row 5) has score entries.

## Verdicts
- C046 HINT2 protocol (row 41): confirms. Protocol only, 138 babies planned, model-derived insulin sensitivity (STAR-GRYPHON), no insulin assay, no results.
- C047 Endocrine Society 2017 (row 16): confirms. Rec 1.7 "We recommend against measuring insulin concentrations when evaluating children or adolescents for obesity." Fasting-based insulin; grade 1, moderate.
- C054 Salehi 2018 (row 5): adds nuance. Full text exists (rows say empty). The diagnostic criteria are history, venous glucose below 54 mg/dL with symptoms, and no hypoglycaemia after a 12-hour fast; insulin is not a criterion, "there are no normative data in this situation", C-peptide is not mentioned, mixed-meal test has no accepted standard, OGTT should play no role, CGM not for diagnosis. Expert review, not a guideline.
- C057 Herold 2019 (row 30): confirms. Prespecified subgroup, OGTT C-peptide below median 1.75 nmol/L, HR 0.19 (0.08 to 0.47); n=76; research-only.
- C065 Pituitary Society 2021 (row 39): adds nuance. Meta-analysis (13 studies, 435): HOMA-IR and fasting insulin improved on pegvisomant alone; only fasting insulin improved with somatostatin ligands.
- C066 HIVMA/IDSA 2020 (row 44): confirms. Glucose and HbA1c only; insulin appears once, as "insulin-sensitizing agents" for treatment.
- C069 Knopp 2021 (row 41): confirms. Single-centre retrospective, 1147 episodes, model-derived SI (non-insulin surrogate), fell 2012 to 2015.
