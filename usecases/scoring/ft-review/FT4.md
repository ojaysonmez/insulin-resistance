# FT4 full-text review (rows 18, 45)

All five texts are good (PMC body text; figures and supplements not read). No score change is proposed; the evidence stays research-only for both rows.

## Per paper
- C113 Renklint 2024 (row 18): adds nuance. Method validation against IVGTT or clamp; I2 87% for HOMA-beta, 82% for IGI(30); prediabetes HOMA-beta r 0.46 (0.30 to 0.60). The 0.48 and 5362 in row 18 were not in the body text. It correlates with first-phase secretion and informs no clinical decision.
- C117 Nie 2025 (row 18): adds nuance. n=9,452, one centre, cross-sectional. The glucose curve looks like I-IFG, but IGI30 equals I-IGT; the disposition-index cut-off 48.5 comes from 546 at-risk outpatients (partly circular). Post-load insulin, Roche Cobas e601, CV 2.2/2.7%.
- C018 Retnakaran 2010 (row 45): confirms. Prospective, n=487; adiponectin predicts postpartum Matsuda and IGI/HOMA-IR; no insulin-guided decision.
- C021 Retnakaran 2011 (row 45): adds nuance. n=166, 3 to 12 months; 1/HOMA-IR change OR 0.13; fasting C-peptide:insulin OR 0.95. Authors only "raise the possibility" of a marker.
- C059 Miao 2020 (row 45): confirms. n=321, single OGTT, 71.7% declined follow-up; antepartum 2-h glucose OR 2.15 is the predictor; HOMA-IR not tested as a predictor.

## Proposals
None. Row 18 has no U/V scores (excluded as a research endpoint). Row 45 D2 (1) and D8 (0) stay.

## Claim issues

- Row 18, Renklint: add heterogeneity caveat; 0.48 and 5362 not verifiable in the body text.
- Row 45: C018 and C021 are longitudinal, not cross-sectional; only C059 is; C021 includes a fasting C-peptide ratio.
