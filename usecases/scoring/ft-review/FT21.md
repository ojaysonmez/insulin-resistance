## Per paper

- **C122 Izumo 2025 (row 22: S4 D1/D2/D3/D7, V12 D6): adds nuance.** One negative-imaging adult nesidioblastosis case. Calcium-stimulated insulin (SACI, positive if more than double basal) stepped up only in the gastroduodenal artery; head resection helped for about a year, then the remnant needed resection. No SACI complications reported. Case report, level 1.
- **C129 Sweatt 2026 (row 48: S3 D2, V21 D2, V23 D1, V24 D1): adds nuance.** 69 healthy non-diabetic adults with obesity (18 RH in Results, 19 in limitations), not patients. RH defined by meal-test glucose curve only; symptoms not recorded; cut-off from the cohort median; insulin appears only in the IVGTT (higher insulin sensitivity and disposition index, no higher acute insulin response). The reasons' "glucose and symptoms" does not apply to this paper. Scores stay (Hall 2021 also feeds them).
- **C130 Yoshida 2026 (row 22): adds nuance.** Imaging-positive head insulinoma localised by exendin-4 PET/CT and PET/MRI; SACST done afterwards (SMA territory positive, GDA negative). Not a negative-imaging case; it is a case for a non-invasive competitor. The 100% vs 89% (SACST) figures are second-hand.
- **C193 Teale 2004 (row 21 V15 D2): confirms.** Eight NICTH cases; insulin and C-peptide suppressed during hypoglycaemia; paper says the IGF ratio is the principal diagnostic parameter; treatment-focused.
- **C205 Geller 2014 (row 3 V1 D7): confirms (context only).** 97,648 insulin-related ED visits a year; sulfonylurea in 39.2% of visits with an oral agent documented. Insulin-treated patients only, no sulfonylurea-only estimate.
- **C222 Pan 2016 (no score entry): not relevant.** Fasting plasma glucose only (5,135 Chinese patients; DM 30.9%, IFG 8.0%); no insulin or C-peptide; no claim cites it.
- **C228 Echouffo-Tcheugui 2018 (row 13 V7 D7): confirms.** Diabetes 29.6% (alternative definition 32.3%) among 409,060 US ischaemic stroke patients aged 65 or older; authors say undiagnosed diabetes was probably missed. 68-70% without diabetes sits inside the 65-80% range; bin 4 holds.
