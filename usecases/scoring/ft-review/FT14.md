Scope: full text of C058, C091, C100, C121, C186, C256, C274, C050 against the score entries on rows 7, 10, 29, 33 and 35. Details are in `usecases/scoring/ft-review/FT14.json`. Only two low-confidence score changes are proposed (row 10, D1).

## Verdicts per paper

- **C058 Elzahar 2020 (PMID 32052124, DOI 10.1007/s00431-020-03606-7), text partial (Table 1 garbled): adds nuance.** Numbers match the abstract (UCPCR 0.28 nmol/nmol, 97%/88%, AUC 0.974; n=80). Already-diagnosed children, single centre, labels not antibody- or gene-verified, groups differ in BMI, research-type ELISA kit. Quote: "Differentiation between MODY type and T2DM was not performed." So it is not a MODY source (row 7) and supports row 33 D2=2, D8=2, D1=2 unchanged. Measure: post-meal urinary C-peptide:creatinine ratio, plus fasting and postprandial serum C-peptide.
- **C091 ISPAD 2022 (PMID 36537518, DOI 10.1111/pedi.13426), good: confirms.** Detectable C-peptide, blood or urine, "at least 5 years after diagnosis" is one suspicion feature among several, none pathognomonic; no recommendation to measure. D3 on row 33 stays 2; ISPAD alone would give 2 on row 7 (row 7 stays 3 on PMDI and ANZ).
- **C100 PMDI 2023 (PMID 37794142, DOI 10.1038/s43856-023-00369-8), good: confirms.** Level 1 evidence for testing under 30 y, non-obese, "autoantibody negative and/or have retained C-peptide levels". C-peptide is an alternative to antibodies, with no threshold. PPV 20% for the biomarker pathway is in the text (the earlier "could not be traced" note can be closed). Limits: selection bias, false negatives not countable.
- **C121 ANZ 2025 (PMID 40640995, DOI 10.5694/mja2.52717), good: confirms.** [Strong] recommendation names "a retained serum C-peptide response to hyperglycaemia" with negative antibodies; no threshold. For GDM only glucose-based GCK testing; nothing on HOMA-IR, insulin or C-peptide in pregnancy, so it supports the row 35 negative (D3=1) but is not a source for the part (b) scores V17 D1, V18 D1, D5, D6, S.
- **C186 Knupp 2026 (PMID 41086125, DOI 10.2337/dc25-1029), good: adds nuance.** AUC 0.98 vs 0.80 vs biomarkers alone 0.96 confirmed. The biomarker term merges C-peptide with antibodies (not separable); external validation had only 5 cases among 940; White European only. D8=3 and D2=3 stand.
- **C256 ProDiGY (PMID 34362814, DOI 10.2337/dc21-0491), good: adds nuance.** 93/3,333 (2.8%) confirmed, in a cohort already selected for antibody-negative and C-peptide above 0.6 ng/mL. Fasting C-peptide AUC 0.67 does not identify MODY in presumed T2D youth.
- **C274 SEARCH incidence (PMID 36868256, DOI 10.1016/s2213-8587(23)00025-6), partial (no tables): contradicts a label.** 18,169 T1D and 5,293 T2D are 2002-2018 registry totals, not annual counts. 2017-2018 rates 22.2 and 17.9 per 100,000. The row 29 and 33 age counts (179, 341, 445, 194) could not be checked.
- **C050 Igls 2018 (PMID 29453879, DOI 10.1111/tri.13138), good: adds nuance.** Every non-failed tier needs a documented C-peptide rise over the pretransplant value; a minimum "above 0.5 ng/ml or 0.17 nmol/l" is stated in the goal and conclusion; fasting with glucose, with or without stimulation; the authors call it a work in progress.
