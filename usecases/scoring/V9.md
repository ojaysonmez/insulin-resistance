# V9 report: D2, D8 and path to viability for rows 1, 2, 24, 29, 22, 23, 33

Role: biostatistician. Coverage and reimbursement out of scope; US only. No measure here is an insulin-resistance index. Origin and read level are given per score. Full detail is also in `usecases/scoring/V9.json`.

## Scores
- Row 1 D2 = 3 (confidence medium, origin both). Two guidelines (Endocrine Society 2009, ENETS 2016) recommend insulin, C-peptide, proinsulin and BOHB at documented hypoglycaemia; the guideline grades the evidence low or very low, and no randomised or outcome study of measurement-guided care was found. Scored 3 as guideline-backed consensus, at the lower edge. Sources: PMID 19088155 DOI 10.1210/jc.2008-1410 abstract; PMID 26742109 (PMC4849884) full text, DOI not re-checked in this unit; project file usecases-verified.md row 1.
- Row 1 D8 = 4 (confidence medium, origin both). Glucose, imaging and clinical features cannot show endogenous hyperinsulinism; insulin plus C-peptide at hypoglycaemia is the only biochemical test that does (proinsulin and BOHB are adjuncts). Judged from the mechanism and guideline logic, not from a reclassification rate (rule 19 would cap at 2 on mechanism alone; 4 chosen because no alternative exists, see anchor_issues). Sources: PMID 19088155 DOI 10.1210/jc.2008-1410 abstract; usecases-verified.md row 1.
- Row 2 D2 = 2 (confidence low, origin both). PES 2015 and Endocrine Society 2009 endorse the insulin-high, C-peptide-suppressed pattern, but sensitivity and specificity of the pattern were not retrieved; the evidence is mechanistic plus case reports. Cohort accuracy data not found in the screened records. Sources: PMID 25957977 PMC11891912 full text (via project file usecases-verified.md row 2; DOI not re-checked); PMID 19088155 DOI 10.1210/jc.2008-1410 abstract; PMID 39086570 DOI 10.4103/ijem.ijem_56_24 title only (no abstract in PubMed).
- Row 2 D8 = 2 (confidence low, origin both). The pattern separates exogenous from endogenous hyperinsulinism where drug screen, insulin analogue-specific LC-MS/MS or antibody tests are alternatives and are partly needed because immunoassays can miss analogues. No data showing the measurement changes the answer given those alternatives; mechanism only, so 2. Sources: PMID 39030378 DOI 10.1007/s12020-024-03970-6 title only; PMID 36733550 DOI 10.7759/cureus.33224 title only; PMID 25957977 (full text per project file).
- Row 24 D2 = 2 (confidence medium, origin both). The international HI guideline (Rec 1.4, Grade 1, low-quality evidence) applies the adult-style insulin and C-peptide work-up to children older than 2 years; the child-specific evidence is small surgical series (title only), so the support is extrapolated adult consensus. Sources: PMID 37454648 DOI 10.1159/000531766 abstract; Rec 1.4 from project file usecases-verified.md row 24; PMID 24314196 DOI 10.1016/j.jpedsurg.2013.04.022 title only.
- Row 24 D8 = 3 (confidence low, origin both). Biochemical proof of hyperinsulinism has no imaging or genetic substitute and, with MEN1 clues, directs the path away from congenital HI work-up. No reclassification data; 3 rests on guideline text that makes the test the entry step. Confidence low because this is not an accuracy comparison. Sources: PMID 37454648 DOI 10.1159/000531766 abstract; PMID 26742109 (ENETS 2016, full text per project file).
- Row 22 D2 = 2 (confidence medium, origin both). ENETS 2016 endorses calcium-stimulated hepatic venous insulin sampling (positive 90-100%) from two small series; a later 80-patient surgical cohort found arterial stimulation venous sampling sensitivity 65% (n=20) and percutaneous transhepatic sampling 90% (n=10), so accuracy is variable and observational only. Sources: PMID 26742109 full text per project file usecases-verified.md row 22; PMID 31369096 DOI 10.1210/jc.2019-01204 abstract.
- Row 22 D8 = 2 (confidence medium, origin new search). Useful in the defined subgroup with negative imaging: in the 80-patient series invasive methods localised the tumour in 7 of 7 patients with 14 non-visualised tumours that had them. No head-to-head against newer imaging (for example exendin PET) was screened. Sources: PMID 31369096 DOI 10.1210/jc.2019-01204 abstract; PMID 33787590 DOI 10.1097/md.0000000000025076 title only.
- Row 23 D2 = 2 (confidence medium, origin both). Observational accuracy only: a 37-case, 42-control study gave AUC 0.97, sensitivity 86.5%, specificity 95.2% for OGTT insulin and C-peptide ratios; a systematic review found two case-control studies (106 patients) at risk of bias. ENETS quotes single-centre data. Cases were confirmed insulinoma, so applicability to fast-negative patients is untested. Sources: PMID 32529519 DOI 10.1007/s10620-020-06379-z abstract; PMID 38863915 DOI 10.15605/jafes.039.01.16 abstract; PMID 26742109 full text per project file.
- Row 23 D8 = 2 (confidence low, origin new search). Useful in the defined subgroup where the fast is negative or not feasible; alternatives are an extended or mixed-meal test. The studies used the 72-hour fast as the reference, not fast-negative patients, so added value in that subgroup is not shown. Sources: PMID 38863915 DOI 10.15605/jafes.039.01.16 abstract; PMID 32529519 DOI 10.1007/s10620-020-06379-z abstract.
- Row 29 D2 = 3 (confidence medium, origin both). The FDA label sets peak stimulated C-peptide >=0.2 pmol/mL on MMTT (plus an islet autoantibody) as an eligibility criterion; approval rests on a surrogate (C-peptide decline at week 78). In PROTECT, 217 vs 111 patients, difference 0.13 pmol/mL (95% CI 0.09-0.17); 94.9% vs 79.2% kept peak C-peptide >=0.2. Surrogate approval scores 3 (clarification 9). Selection by C-peptide was not itself randomised (clarification 8 would cap at 2; see anchor_issues). Sources: PMID 37861217 DOI 10.1056/nejmoa2308743 abstract; FDA Tzield label rev 06/2026 s2.1, s14.2 per project file usecases-verified.md row 29; PMID 42720752 DOI 10.1007/s00125-026-06849-5 title only.
- Row 29 D8 = 2 (confidence low, origin both). Stimulated C-peptide identifies residual beta-cell function; alternatives are autoantibodies (also required) and time since diagnosis. No retrieved analysis shows C-peptide selection improves outcome beyond those, so mechanism only, 2. Sources: PMID 37861217 DOI 10.1056/nejmoa2308743 abstract; FDA Tzield label per project file.
- Row 33 D2 = 2 (confidence high, origin both). Three small single-centre cohorts with ROC accuracy: UCPCR >=0.7 nmol/mmol, sensitivity 100% (95% CI 92-100), specificity 97% (91-99), n=264 after 2 years; UCPCR >=0.22, AUC 0.93, n=69; UCPCR >=0.28, sensitivity 97%, specificity 88%, n=80. Cutoffs differ 3-fold, each data-derived and not externally validated. No outcome or management study. Sources: PMID 23289766 DOI 10.1111/pedi.12008 abstract; PMID 25792383 DOI 10.1159/000375410 abstract; PMID 32052124 DOI 10.1007/s00431-020-03606-7 abstract.
- Row 33 D8 = 2 (confidence low, origin new search). Defined subgroup (diabetes >2 years, antibody status unclear) where clinical features misclassify; genetic testing and autoantibodies are alternatives and the abstracts report no comparison or incremental AUC against them. Fasting C-peptide did worse than UCPCR in one cohort (sensitivity 63%). Sources: PMID 32052124 DOI 10.1007/s00431-020-03606-7 abstract; PMID 25792383 DOI 10.1159/000375410 abstract.

## Path to viability
- Row 1 (evidence, effort medium): A prospective accuracy study with a standard assay-specific cutoff set (current insulin and C-peptide immunoassays) in consecutive patients with Whipple's triad. Basis: Thresholds are second-hand and the guideline grades the evidence low or very low (PMID 19088155).
- Row 2 (assay, effort medium): Report analogue cross-reactivity per assay with a validated cutoff pattern, and a cohort accuracy study of insulin plus C-peptide against drug screen or LC-MS/MS. Basis: Immunoassays vary in analogue detection (PMID 39086570, 39030378, 36733550 titles only); no cohort accuracy found.
- Row 24 (evidence, effort medium): A paediatric series of insulin and C-peptide at hypoglycaemia in children over 2 years with confirmed insulinoma or MEN1, with age-specific cutoffs. Basis: The guideline recommendation is Grade 1 on low-quality evidence (PMID 37454648).
- Row 22 (evidence, effort medium): A head-to-head cohort of calcium-stimulated venous sampling against exendin PET/CT in negative-imaging patients, with surgical cure as the outcome. Basis: Evidence is small series; sensitivity ranged 65-100% (PMID 31369096, ENETS 2016).
- Row 23 (evidence, effort medium): A prospective study of OGTT or mixed-meal insulin and C-peptide ratios in patients with postprandial symptoms and a negative fast, with an external validation cohort. Basis: Existing models were fit on confirmed cases versus controls, n=37/42 (PMID 32529519), and the systematic review judged them at risk of bias (PMID 38863915).
- Row 29 (evidence, effort large): Clinical-outcome follow-up of PROTECT (HbA1c, insulin dose, hypoglycaemia) and a subgroup analysis by baseline C-peptide showing selection adds to antibody status. Basis: Approval is on a surrogate; ADA 2026 does not endorse the stage 3 indication (project file row 29).
- Row 33 (evidence, effort medium): A multi-centre validation of a single UCPCR cutoff against genetic and autoantibody-defined type, with a comparison to autoantibody testing alone. Basis: Three single-centre cohorts with cutoffs 0.22 to 0.7 (PMID 23289766, 25792383, 32052124).
