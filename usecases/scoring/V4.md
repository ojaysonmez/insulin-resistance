## Reasoning per row

**Row 31.** D1 3: the random C-peptide with glucose determines whether insulin deficiency is present and so whether insulin can be stopped or avoided; clinical features and antibodies partly substitute and nothing makes it mandatory (Holt 2021 PMID 34590174; Jones and Hattersley 2013 PMID 23413806; full texts per project files). D5 3: one non-fasting draw, orderable in primary care or by an endocrinologist. D6 2: burden 4, false-result 2 (a falsely high value from renal impairment or too soon after hyperglycaemia could lead to unsafe insulin withdrawal; a falsely low one keeps insulin, the default). S 1: default is continue insulin, harm is unnecessary insulin. Confidence medium to low. Funakoshi 2011 (PMID 24843502, DOI 10.1111/j.2040-1124.2010.00096.x, abstract) concerns who needs insulin to reach target, not safe withdrawal.

**Row 7.** D1 2: C-peptide or post-meal UCPCR is one triage component (PMDI 2023 PMID 37794142; ANZ consensus 2025 PMID 40640995; full texts per project files); the pathway PPV was 20% and UCPCR cannot separate MODY from T2D. D5 2: timed urine sample, specialist work-up. D6 3: burden 3, false-result 3 (false positive gives a negative gene test; false negative leaves the missed diagnosis, reversible). S 1: age, BMI and antibody criteria would still trigger many tests; could be 2 if C-peptide were the only triage.

**Row 3.** D1 2: the pattern shows hyperinsulinaemia only; it is the same for insulinoma, and the drug screen decides (Cryer 2009 PMID 19088155; PES 2015 PMID 25957977, no PubMed abstract). D5 2: timed sample at a hypoglycaemic event, specialist-led, inpatient or emergency. D6 2: burden 2 with a provoking fast, false-result 3. S 3: unrecognised recurrent sulfonylurea hypoglycaemia can mean hospitalisation or brain injury; low confidence because the drug screen and history also point to the cause.

**Row 32.** D1 3: post-DKA beta-cell function with antibodies predicts long-term insulin dependence (Balasubramanyam 2006, PMID 17130187, DOI 10.2337/dc06-0749, abstract: n=294, 12 to 60 months follow-up); antibodies partly substitute. D5 2: specialist follow-up weeks after DKA. D6 2: false-result 2 (early withdrawal and recurrent DKA). S 1 under the continue-insulin default; 3 if empirical withdrawal were the default.

**Row 47.** D1 3: fasting C-peptide below 0.6 ng/mL is one of three criteria for a definitive diagnosis in the Japan Diabetes Society 2023 criteria (PMID 38184802, DOI 10.1111/jdi.14121, abstract re-fetched); national criteria elsewhere score 3 (clarification 12), and the US effect is smaller. D5 2, D6 3, S 1: insulin would be started on glycaemic grounds anyway.

**Row 49.** D1 1: prognosis for monitoring intensity only (clarification 11); no recommendation to test and no treatment selection. D5 1: Index60 and the minimal model need 5 to 9 timed samples, research-network use. D6 2: burden 3; false-result 2 (false reassurance delays surveillance); thresholds are cohort-specific medians. S 1: glucose-based monitoring continues regardless. Sources: Ismail 2022 PMID 35998266 DOI 10.2337/dc22-0167 (abstract); Galderisi 2025 PMID 39992257 DOI 10.1210/clinem/dgaf086 (AUC C-peptide not significant in multivariable model, full-text windows per project files); Insel 2015 PMID 26404926 DOI 10.2337/dc15-1419. This is a post-load measure, which the project evaluated less.

**Row 4.** D1 2: insulin and C-peptide at glucose <50 mg/dL are the guideline's "less definitive" criteria; BOHB, FFA and glucagon response are the reliable markers; insulin is often undetectable (HI guideline 2023 PMID 37454648, DOI 10.1159/000531766; PES 2015 PMID 25957977). D5 2: paediatric endocrinology, critical sample in hospital. D6 1: burden 2 (infant blood volume, possible fast), false-result 1 (an undetectable insulin that is wrongly relied on delays diagnosis; guidelines mitigate). S 3 (not 4) because other markers reach the diagnosis without insulin.

**Row 10.** D1 3: Igls requires an increase over pre-transplant C-peptide for optimal graft function and calls pre-transplant testing critical (PMC5867272 keyword windows; PMIDs 29528967, 29453879, 34061967); the criteria name no treatment action. D5 2: transplant-centre follow-up. D6 2: renal dysfunction raises C-peptide and low values are of questionable significance with sensitive assays. S 2: moderate, delayed recognition of graft decline. Note: Igls 2.0 not found in the files; no numeric threshold for clinically significant C-peptide.
