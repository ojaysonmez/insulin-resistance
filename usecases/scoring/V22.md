## Evidence base (all abstract level)
- Insulin immunoassays: ADA workgroup, PMID 17272483, DOI 10.1373/clinchem.2006.082214. Within-assay CV 3.7% to 39.0%; among-assay CV 12% to 66%, median 24%; a common reference preparation did not improve agreement. 2007-era platforms.
- PMID 19325009, DOI 10.1373/clinchem.2008.118380: against an IDMS reference, bias over 15.5% for 7 of 10 methods. 2009-era platforms; may not apply to current assays.
- C-peptide: PMID 17332147, DOI 10.1373/clinchem.2006.081570: 10 methods, 15 laboratories; between-laboratory variability rose with concentration; WHO reagent normalization did not help, sample-based normalization did. PMID 40781799, DOI 10.1177/19322968251362848 (2025 review): ADA/EASD C-peptide cutoffs (0.2 and 0.6 nmol/L) limited because assays are not standardized. PMID 38549041, DOI 10.1093/clinchem/hvae034: antibody-free LC-MS/MS for insulin and C-peptide, 2.7% to 3.7% CV within consortium laboratories; a research route to harmonization, not a US routine test.
- Antibody interference: PMID 27588366 (DOI 10.1111/cen.13179): five assays, PEG lowered recovery on all but one; PMID 39071705 (DOI 10.1016/j.heliyon.2024.e34009): three assays gave very different insulin results in one patient; PMID 25319986 (DOI 10.1309/ajcpx56jqbjhubgj): antibodies and an M-protein interfered with insulin and C-peptide; PEG gives free C-peptide. All case-level.
- MMTT: PMID 38950997 (DOI 10.1136/bmjopen-2024-084808) and PMID 32169925 (DOI 10.1136/bmjopen-2019-033500): 2-h MMTT C-peptide as trial primary outcomes. PMID 35019897 (DOI 10.1097/tp.0000000000004058): transplant monitoring not standardized.

## Per-row reasoning
- Row 20 (2): insulin and C-peptide assays widely available but unstandardized and assay-dependent under antibody interference; PEG is lab-developed, IAA often send-out. Alternative 1 if the add-on steps define the test.
- Row 45 (2, low): no insulin test in the pathway; scored the assay a fasting insulin or HOMA-IR use would need. Comparator OGTT and A1C would be 3 to 4.
- Row 11 (2): fasting C-peptide is routine to order, but cutoffs are study-specific and assays disagree; invalid with insulin therapy or CKD (project files).
- Row 8 (2): unstandardized assay plus timed multi-sample protocol, run in trial settings.
- Row 25 (1, low): small infant sample, paired maternal sample, lab-developed PEG, no validated protocol found; evidence is a 2026 narrative review (PMID 42697708, DOI 10.1177/00045632261489517) and case reports.
- Row 27 (2, low): MMTT at few high-volume centres, non-standardized monitoring. Alternative 1.
- Row 48 (2, low): long timed OGTT or MMTT, no standard glucose cutoff, plus an unstandardized insulin immunoassay.
