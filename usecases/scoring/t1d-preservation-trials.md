# Beta-cell preservation trials in type 1 diabetes: C-peptide outcomes and enrolment (registry count)

Status: registry search, done 2026-10-25 by an evidence-search subagent for the Metsulin-fit view (`usecases/scoring/metsulin-fit.md`, candidates N1, N2 and N6). Budget: at most 10 searches or look-ups; 8 used. Retrieval and documentation only; no grading. Stem-cell-derived islet trials are not repeated here (see `usecases/scoring/stem-cell-islet-trials.md`); islet, pancreas and other cell-replacement trials are excluded.

Measure type: C-peptide (serum or plasma, venous, measured during a stimulation test or fasting, at a clinic or central laboratory) unless a row says otherwise. DBS = dried blood spot (capillary). Insulin is not an outcome in any included record in a way relevant here.

Read level: every row is "registry record" only: the ClinicalTrials.gov API v2 fields (title, sponsor, collaborators, status, dates, enrolment, locations, interventions, eligibility, outcome measures), the ISRCTN XML fields, or the CTIS public search fields (title, status code, sponsor, phase, enrolment, countries, end points). No protocol, paper or results section was read. No PMIDs or DOIs are cited, so none needed checking.

## Inclusion rules used (judgement; counts depend on them)

- Interventional trial in people with stage 1 or stage 2 type 1 diabetes, or stage 3 entered within about 12 months of diagnosis (the whole trial, or a defined cohort; three trials with mixed cohorts are flagged and counted in full).
- Intervention aimed at beta-cell preservation or disease modification: immunotherapy, cell therapy (mesenchymal stromal cells, Tregs, CAR-T), repurposed drugs (verapamil, DFMO, dimethyl fumarate, GLP-1 receptor agonists, vitamin D), vaccines, microbiome and diet interventions tested for C-peptide preservation, and early closed-loop insulin only when combined with a preservation agent (CLVer).
- C-peptide named in a registered primary or secondary outcome. Every secondary outcome naming C-peptide was accepted as "key secondary"; the records do not rank secondary outcomes, so this is an upper bound.
- Status: recruiting, not yet recruiting, enrolling by invitation, active not recruiting, or completed with a completion date on or after 2021-01-01. Terminated, suspended, withdrawn and unknown-status trials are listed separately and not counted.
- Excluded: established type 1 diabetes (entry window starting beyond about 12 months or open to any duration; listed separately), islet, pancreas or stem-cell islet replacement, glycaemic-control-only trials (closed loop, adjunct drugs, obesity), MODY, Wolfram syndrome, type 2 diabetes, pregnancy, and primary prevention before stage 1 (for example NCT06670625, infant diet counselling, n=1244 est.).
- Count per trial, not per registration: NCT04509791 = ISRCTN15367345 (MELD-ATG), NCT04545151 = EU CT 2023-506545-27-01 (Ver-A-T1D), and the CTIS records that match ClinicalTrials.gov trials (listed in the search log) were counted once.

## What this means for remote C-peptide sampling

- **Size of the trial population (judgement).** About 70 trials enrolling about 6,400 people fit the scope; about 3,500 people are in trials still recruiting or not yet recruiting, spread over 36 trials, 10 of them industry-led. For a sponsor-facing test, the near-term market is a few dozen protocols a year, each of tens to a few hundred participants, not thousands of patients.
- **Current practice is clinic MMTT.** Nearly every record uses a supervised 2-hour (sometimes 4-hour) mixed-meal tolerance test on venous blood as the C-peptide endpoint, typically at 2 to 5 time points in year 1. OGTT replaces MMTT in stage 1 and 2 trials. A home capillary test would not replace the regulatory endpoint as these records are written; it would add between-visit samples.
- **Remote sampling is rare but already in European network protocols.** Only 3 of 72 trials register blood-based DBS C-peptide, all INNODIA-linked or French academic; MELD-ATG asked for monthly DBS for 12 months, and Ver-A-T1D adds DBS at weeks 4 and 8 and at the 3-monthly visits. One Dutch trial uses home urinary C-peptide. None of the industry phase 2-3 trials (teplizumab, baricitinib, frexalimab, brivekimig, SAB-142) registers any home, capillary or DBS C-peptide. The metsulin-fit candidate N6 (decentralised trial sampling) is therefore supported as a fit for academic network trials today and remains untested in industry trials (judgement).
- **Frequency.** Where stated, registered C-peptide time points are below the 5-10 a year threshold in most trials (34 of 44 with a stated schedule have 4 or fewer). The two DBS trials with stated schedules exceed it (about 7 and about 16 a year), which is the use pattern a home test would serve.
- **Gaps for a home test (from the records, not tested here).** Stimulated values are the endpoint; a home test would need a home stimulus (standard meal or drink) or a validated fasting or random measure that tracks MMTT AUC. Low-end performance near 0.2 nmol/L peak (the "C-peptide positive" threshold named in 8 of the ClinicalTrials.gov outcome records) matters most. ISRCTN18189469 (Oxford, capillary glucose during OGTT in children with ≥2 islet antibodies, n=49 est.) is the only registry record found that validates capillary OGTT sampling in early type 1 diabetes; it registers capillary glucose, not C-peptide, in the fields read.
