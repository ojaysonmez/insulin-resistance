## Row 6
Setting: specialist outpatient

- **D1 = 3** (medium, both): Random C-peptide with concurrent glucose, in insulin-treated people more than 3 years from diagnosis, decides whether insulin deficiency is absolute (below 80 pmol/L), whether insulin can be replaced (600 pmol/L or more), and whether to send MODY genetics. It determines classification and treatment but is not a required step: islet autoantibodies are the primary test at diagnosis. Measure type: C-peptide, random. Sources: PMID 34590174 DOI 10.1007/s00125-021-05568-3 abstract (full text per usecases-verified.md row 6, skeptic-read); PMID 23413806 DOI 10.1111/dme.12159 abstract (full text per usecases-verified.md row 6); usecases-verified.md row 6 (Holt thresholds, 11% reclassified)
- **D5 = 3** (medium, project files): A single venous sample with a paired glucose, standard laboratory test that primary care or diabetes clinics can order; the limits are the timing rules (not in first 3 years, not within 2 weeks of hyperglycaemic emergency, glucose not below 4 mmol/L, renal impairment) and interpretation of the 200-600 pmol/L band. Scored 3 (routine laboratory) rather than 4 because it is not part of a standing visit panel. Sources: PMID 34590174 DOI 10.1007/s00125-021-05568-3 abstract (full text per usecases-verified.md row 6, skeptic-read); PMID 23413806 DOI 10.1111/dme.12159 abstract (full text per usecases-verified.md row 6); usecases-verified.md row 6
- **D6 = 3** (medium, project files): Burden is one blood draw. Main risk is a false-low result in invalid conditions (very recent diagnosis, low glucose, renal impairment, recent ketoacidosis) leading to a wrong T1D label, or a false-high one leading to insulin withdrawal in an insulin-deficient person; the consensus rules (no repeat below 80, caveats) limit this. Low risk, not negligible. Sources: PMID 34590174 DOI 10.1007/s00125-021-05568-3 abstract (full text per usecases-verified.md row 6, skeptic-read); PMID 23413806 DOI 10.1111/dme.12159 abstract (full text per usecases-verified.md row 6); usecases-verified.md row 6 (invalid conditions)
- **S = 2** (low, project files): Without the result, insulin-treated people may stay on insulin that is not needed (hypoglycaemia, burden) or MODY and non-insulin options may be missed: moderate, delayed harm. The life-threatening harm (DKA) arises from a wrong result or wrong insulin withdrawal, not from absent information, because insulin-treated patients keep taking insulin by default. Sources: PMID 34590174 DOI 10.1007/s00125-021-05568-3 abstract (full text per usecases-verified.md row 6, skeptic-read); PMID 23413806 DOI 10.1111/dme.12159 abstract (full text per usecases-verified.md row 6)

## Row 5
Setting: specialist outpatient

- **D1 = 2** (medium, project files): Insulin (with C-peptide and glucose) during a spontaneous episode or at the glucose nadir of a mixed-meal test supports the diagnosis of postprandial hyperinsulinaemic hypoglycaemia and helps exclude insulinoma, drugs and exogenous insulin, but the project files record no accepted insulin threshold and the diagnosis rests on documented low glucose with symptoms. Management (diet, acarbose, other drugs) does not clearly depend on the insulin value. Measure type: post-load or mixed-meal insulin, plus spontaneous-episode sample. Sources: PMID 30101281 DOI 10.1210/jc.2018-00528 abstract (full text empty per usecases-verified.md row 5); PMID 34802853 DOI 10.1016/j.numecd.2021.08.047 abstract; usecases-verified.md row 5
- **D5 = 1** (medium, project files): Mixed-meal test lasting hours with timed glucose and insulin samples, or an opportunistic critical sample, interpreted by an endocrinologist; patients are typically referred. Scored 1 (specialist, complex protocol); a spontaneous sample during an observed episode would be 2. Sources: PMID 30101281 DOI 10.1210/jc.2018-00528 abstract (full text empty per usecases-verified.md row 5); usecases-verified.md row 5
- **D6 = 2** (medium, project files): The provocative meal test can cause symptomatic or neuroglycopenic hypoglycaemia in patients described as having falls, accidents and seizures, so it needs supervision and rescue glucose. A spontaneous sample is low burden. Moderate overall. Sources: PMID 30101281 DOI 10.1210/jc.2018-00528 abstract (full text empty per usecases-verified.md row 5) (severity of events); PMID 34802853 DOI 10.1016/j.numecd.2021.08.047 abstract
- **S = 3** (medium, both): Post-bariatric hypoglycaemia can be severe and disabling (neuroglycopenia, falls, motor vehicle accidents, seizures, loss of consciousness); a missed alternative cause (insulinoma, exogenous insulin) or missed diagnosis keeps this risk. Serious morbidity but usually not life-threatening. Sources: PMID 30101281 DOI 10.1210/jc.2018-00528 abstract (full text empty per usecases-verified.md row 5); PMID 34802853 DOI 10.1016/j.numecd.2021.08.047 abstract

## Row 13
Setting: specialist outpatient

- **D1 = 2** (medium, both): HOMA-IR above 3.0 was the IRIS entry criterion, so it defines the evidence-based group in which pioglitazone cut stroke or MI (HR 0.76). ADA 2026 Rec 3.13 and AACE 2022 R 9.4 say evidence of insulin resistance without naming an assay or threshold, and pioglitazone is also an accepted option in prediabetes after stroke, so the result chooses among accepted options at best. Whether benefit differs by HOMA-IR stratum is not shown in the sources read. Measure type: fasting-based (HOMA-IR). Sources: PMID 26886418 DOI 10.1056/nejmoa1506930 abstract plus PMC4887756 keyword-grep windows; PMID 35963508 DOI 10.1016/j.eprac.2022.08.002 abstract (guideline text per usecases-verified.md row 13, skeptic-read verbatim); ADA Standards of Care 2026 s3 Rec 3.13 (per usecases-verified.md row 13)
- **D5 = 1** (low, project files): Needs a fasting insulin and glucose (HOMA-IR is computed), insulin is not standardised so IRIS used central laboratories, and no clear owner exists (neurology, primary care or endocrinology after the 14-day window). No routine pathway; scored 1 because the test itself is simple but the assay is unstandardised and ownership is unclear. Sources: PMID 26886418 DOI 10.1056/nejmoa1506930 abstract plus PMC4887756 keyword-grep windows; usecases-verified.md row 13 (central laboratories, unstandardised insulin)
- **D6 = 3** (medium, both): Burden is one fasting draw. A false-high result exposes a patient to pioglitazone, whose harms in IRIS include weight gain above 4.5 kg and more discontinuation for oedema or weight gain (172 vs 51), heart failure, fracture and bladder-cancer surveillance; a false-low result withholds a benefit of about 2.8 percentage points over 4.8 years. Consequences are moderate and reversible, test burden low. Sources: PMID 26886418 DOI 10.1056/nejmoa1506930 abstract plus PMC4887756 keyword-grep windows
- **S = 2** (low, both): IRIS absolute reduction in stroke or MI was 2.8 points over 4.8 years (9.0% vs 11.8%), so missing the selection means delayed, moderate harm; clinicians can also give pioglitazone to these patients without a HOMA-IR value, so the marginal harm from lacking the measurement is smaller than the harm of recurrent stroke itself. Recurrent stroke is serious, so a 3 is arguable. Sources: PMID 26886418 DOI 10.1056/nejmoa1506930 abstract plus PMC4887756 keyword-grep windows
