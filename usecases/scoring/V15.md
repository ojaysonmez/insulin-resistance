## Reasoning and sources

**Row 14.** D2 1: EASL-EASD-EASO 2024 (PMIDs 38851997, 38852583, 38869512, full text per project files) gives a weak "may be considered" (LoE 3); AASLD 2023 (PMID 36727674) is silent; no pathway step or therapy depends on the result. HOMA-IR appears as a component of fibrosis scores (PMID 33333040, DOI 10.1016/j.cca.2020.12.012, abstract; AUROC 0.78 to 0.82), which is accuracy, not decision impact. D8 1 (alternative 0): FIB-4, elastography and glucose/HbA1c drive the pathway; the Delphi consensus (PMID 37364790) excludes HOMA-IR for cost and between-laboratory variability. No increment over FIB-4 found in the screened records.

**Row 9.** D2 1: PMID 39959260 (DOI 10.4239/wjd.v16.i2.95102, abstract) is a 44 vs 35 single-centre comparison (fasting C-peptide 0.81 vs 1.71 nmol/L); PancreasFest 2012 (PMID 23890130) diagnoses 3c without C-peptide. D8 1 (alternative 2 if a TPIAT study shows C-peptide changes selection; not found).

**Row 28.** D2 2: PMID 36465621 (DOI 10.3389/fendo.2022.1052794, abstract) UCPCR <=1.13 nmol/g, sensitivity 88%, specificity 95%, n=85, same-sample cutoff, no external validation (overfitting risk); PMID 30794834 (DOI 10.1016/j.diabres.2019.02.015, abstract) glucose/C-peptide ratio falls with eGFR. No guideline. D8 1: UCPCR is still a C-peptide measure; no comparison with clinical features or antibodies.

**Row 12.** D2 1: JDS 2022 (PMID 35463863, DOI not recorded in the project row; full text per project files) single-nation expert criteria, no accuracy; Brown 2016 (PMID 27710244) names no insulin threshold. D8 2: fasting insulin is the only biochemical major sign, but 89 Japanese T2D adults with low BMI had none above 30 and some genetic cases are below 30; cap 2 by clarification 19.

**Row 21.** D2 1: case reports PMIDs 39219958 (DOI 10.7759/cureus.65825), 36303203 (DOI 10.1186/s12902-022-01175-4), 27905899 (DOI 10.1186/s12885-016-2968-8), 15049960 (DOI 10.1111/j.1365-2265.2004.01989.x), all abstract, show suppressed insulin and C-peptide leading to IGF-II ratio and imaging. D8 2 (alternative 1): suppression is the branch point from hyperinsulinism; BHB and glucagon response overlap; IGF-II confirms.

**Row 35 (part b).** D2 2: PMIDs 26754418 (DOI 10.1007/s40618-015-0427-z; AUC 0.809, 20 cases), 36675721 (DOI 10.3390/jpm13010060; n=1343, AUC 0.733, cutoffs 1.43 to 2.27 by BMI), 34449903 (DOI 10.1002/ijgo.13905), 38017369 (DOI 10.1186/s12884-023-06113-3), abstracts. **Row 46.** D2 1: Imagawa 2012 (PMID 24843620, per project files) expert criteria; PMID 23865326 (no DOI, abstract) 8 patients; PMID 41049521 (DOI 10.1297/cpe.2025-0031, abstract) case report.
