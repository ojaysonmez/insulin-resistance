# Stem-cell-derived islet trials: C-peptide sampling (registry and literature check)

Status: short search, done 2026-10-25 by an evidence-search subagent for the Metsulin-fit view (`usecases/scoring/metsulin-fit.md`, row 10 and candidate N7). Budget: at most 10 searches or look-ups; 9 used. Retrieval and documentation only; no grading.

Scope of the measure: C-peptide (serum or plasma, venous, measured in clinic or central laboratory) unless stated. Approval status of any product: **not checked** (no source read in this search states it).

Read levels: "registry record" = the ClinicalTrials.gov or ISRCTN record fields retrieved through the API (title, sponsor, status, dates, enrolment, countries, interventions, outcome measures). Results sections of registry records were not read.

## What this means for a home C-peptide test

- **Tests per participant per year (trial protocols).** Where a schedule is stated, stimulated C-peptide (MMTT) is measured about every 3 months: 4 per year after implant in VC-02 (PMID 38012450, full text) and in the registered Seraxis protocols (NCT07581197, NCT06651515). VC-02 cohort 2 added shorter interim tests (2-h MMTT or 0 and 60-90 min meal challenge) between full MMTTs (PMID 35028608), so some participants had more than 4 C-peptide test visits a year; the exact count was not stated in the text read. Most other registry records state only a single time frame (day 90, month 6, week 26 or 1 year), so their visit frequency is unknown from the registry. On the stated schedules, stem-cell islet trials sit at about 4 stimulated tests a year, below the 5 to 10 a year threshold. Only the primary-islet UP421 trial (not stem-cell-derived) registers frequent non-fasting C-peptide (16 time points in year 1) (judgement on the threshold comparison).
- **Remote sampling.** None of the 14 stem-cell-derived islet trial records or 4 papers read mention home, capillary or dried-blood-spot C-peptide. All described C-peptide sampling is venous, at a clinic visit, during a supervised MMTT, analysed centrally. CGM is the remote glucose measure in these trials. The one remote C-peptide protocol found (NCT07828626, Exeter) is in people with insulin-treated diabetes, not cell-therapy recipients.
- **Participants enrolled.** Across the 12 interventional stem-cell-derived islet trials with enrolment above zero (excluding the VC-01 follow-up study NCT02939118, whose 51 people come from the VC-01 trials, and the withdrawn NCT06651515): 251 registered participants in total, 114 actual (ViaCyte, CRISPR, VX-264) and 137 estimated (VX-880 x2, CRG-002, Peking/Reprogenix, E-islet 01, SR-02). Including E-islet 01 and SR-02 assumes they are stem-cell-derived, which the registry fields retrieved do not state (unverified).
- **Other endpoints that a test would sit beside.** HbA1c, CGM time in range, insulin dose or independence, and severe hypoglycaemia appear in most efficacy-stage records; insulin independence plus freedom from severe hypoglycaemia is the Phase 3 VX-880 primary endpoint.
