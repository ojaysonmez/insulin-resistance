# Draft list of use cases for insulin and C-peptide measurement (unverified)

Written by the main session from project results and general clinical knowledge. "Source" says which. Rows marked "Common knowledge" have NOT been verified by any run in this project. Status labels are provisional.

| # | Patient type / problem | Test | Provisional status | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Adult with fasting hypoglycaemia: insulinoma vs other causes | Insulin, C-peptide, proinsulin during a supervised fast | Established | Common knowledge; Cryer guideline cited in Set A |
| 2 | Suspected factitious or exogenous insulin hypoglycaemia | High insulin with suppressed C-peptide | Established | Common knowledge |
| 3 | Sulfonylurea or meglitinide hypoglycaemia | Insulin and C-peptide (high) plus drug screen | Established | Common knowledge |
| 4 | Infant or child with hypoglycaemia (congenital hyperinsulinism) | Insulin and C-peptide at hypoglycaemia | Established | Common knowledge |
| 5 | Hypoglycaemia after bariatric surgery | Insulin during symptoms | Established but limited | Common knowledge |
| 6 | Diabetes classification: T1D, T2D, LADA, MODY | C-peptide, with or without antibodies | Established | Project (Set A endocrinologist rated high) |
| 7 | Choosing who gets genetic testing for MODY or neonatal diabetes | C-peptide | Established | Common knowledge |
| 8 | Residual beta-cell function in T1D, including trial endpoints | Stimulated C-peptide | Established in research | Common knowledge |
| 9 | Pancreatogenic (type 3c) diabetes, post-pancreatectomy | C-peptide | Established but limited | Common knowledge |
| 10 | Islet or pancreas transplant graft function | C-peptide | Established | Common knowledge |
| 11 | Bariatric surgery: predicting diabetes remission | C-peptide in remission scores | Supported but no better than diabetes duration alone | Project (PMIDs 22963817, 32873586, abstract-level) |
| 12 | Severe or syndromic IR: lipodystrophy, type A or B IR | Fasting insulin | Specialist use | Project (JDS 2022 criteria, PMID 35463863; lipodystrophy guideline diagnoses by phenotype plus genetics) |
| 13 | Stroke or TIA with prediabetes: pioglitazone selection | HOMA-IR above 3.0 | Embedded in ADA 2026 and AACE 2022 wording; no assay named | Project (IRIS, PMID 26886418) |
| 14 | Suspected MASLD without T2D | HOMA-IR or OGTT estimates | Weak "may be considered" (EASL 2024, level of evidence 3) | Project |
| 15 | PCOS | Insulin, HOMA-IR | Guideline says not for routine care (2023, practice points 1.9.12, 3.1.10) | Project |
| 16 | Paediatric obesity | Insulin | Endocrine Society 2017 Rec 1.7 reportedly recommends against | Project (single-source, unverified) |
| 17 | Individual risk prediction, treatment-response monitoring, or screening | HOMA-IR, TyG | Not supported | Project (noise, assays, cutoffs) |
| 18 | Research: enrolment in IR-targeted trials, IR as an endpoint | HOMA-IR, clamp, OGTT insulin | Research use | Project (registered trials) |
| 19 | Cystic fibrosis related diabetes | Insulin and OGTT indices | Research setting | Project (ADA text via skeptic) |

Context: the project's main conclusion is that routine IR measurement (fasting insulin, HOMA-IR, TyG) has no established clinical utility, while insulin and C-peptide have established roles for other questions (hypoglycaemia, classification, specialist settings). Files are in ir-team/ (merged-conclusion.md is the main synthesis).
