# Revised draft rows: post-meal, post-load and stimulated insulin or C-peptide uses (for skeptic review)

Status: written by the main session on 2026-10-19 from `postprandial-additions-draft.md` (scout, abstract level) with the corrections from `postprandial-fulltext-check.md` (full-text windows read with `tools/pmc_text.py`). Not merged into `usecases-verified.md`, not skeptic-reviewed. Row numbers D1..D6 are temporary; the main session numbers accepted rows 48 onward. Every change from the scout's draft is marked "CHANGED". Read levels: "full-text windows" means passages around search patterns were read, not every page. Coverage and reimbursement are out of scope.

## Changes made to the scout's draft
- D1: added what the full texts say about the 55 mg/dL cut-off and the missing insulin threshold; marked the counts 69 and 19 unverified; Cryer 2009 recorded as unread.
- D2: removed the claim that the oral minimal model separated stage 2 from stage 1 (only phi-total was associated with progression); added the 2015 staging statement and ADA Standards 2026.
- D4: status changed from limited to research-only for the insulin or C-peptide element, because the preoperative predictors found are glucose-based.
- D5: removed two figures that could not be found in the full text.
- D3 and D6: unchanged; abstract level only.
