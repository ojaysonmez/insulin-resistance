# Use-case review: hypoglycaemia, pancreatic disease and transplant

Reviewer: ir-usecase-reviewer (area: HYPOGLYCAEMIA, PANCREATIC DISEASE AND TRANSPLANT). Draft rows checked: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 8, 9, 10. Coverage and reimbursement out of scope. Today: 2026-10-18.

Label convention: every row in this area is a SECRETION, HYPERSECRETION or CLASSIFICATION use. None is an insulin-resistance use. Evidence tags: [full] = full text read; [abs] = abstract only; [title] = title/metadata only; [recall] = from memory, not retrieved.

Source access note: the PubMed connector rewrites free-text queries into long OR/AND expansions, so natural-language searches often returned 0 or off-topic hits. Title-tag and author-tag queries worked better. The Endocrine Society hypoglycaemia guideline (Cryer 2009) has no PMC full text; I read its abstract and read the ENETS 2016 guideline full text, which quotes the Endocrine Society diagnostic thresholds.

## 3. Common knowledge, unsourced (real but not retrieved)

1. 72-hour supervised fast termination rules (glucose <=45 mg/dL with symptoms, etc.) and the 48-hour sensitivity figure [recall].
2. C-peptide suppression test with insulin-induced hypoglycaemia (suppression <50% expected; used to separate endogenous from exogenous insulin in selected cases) [recall].
3. C-peptide accumulates in renal impairment and may mask suppression in factitious insulin use [recall]; the retrieved papers (N9) only support the direction.
4. IGF-II : IGF-I ratio >10 for NICTH [recall].
5. Unreported specific assay cross-reactivity numbers for insulin analogues (some immunoassays detect analogues poorly) [recall]; PES 2015 states the caveat in general terms only.
6. Proinsulin as a discriminator for insulinoma vs factitious use (proinsulin suppressed with exogenous insulin) [recall]; the ES/ENETS thresholds are retrieved but the factitious-use pattern for proinsulin is not.
7. Tolerance of oral glucose provocation in PBH being discouraged, and use of MMTT for PBH diagnosis [recall].
8. Heterophile antibody and macro-insulin interference in insulin assays [recall].
9. Neonates of diabetic mothers and large-for-gestational-age babies as transient hyperinsulinism [recall]; the HI guideline names maternal diabetes and perinatal stress as acquired causes [full].
10. Drug-induced hyperinsulinaemic hypoglycaemia (quinine, pentamidine) [recall].

## 5. What I could not retrieve

- Full text of Cryer 2009 (Endocrine Society guideline): abstract only. Its recommendation grades, the 72-hour fast rules, and the exact wording of thresholds are second-hand via ENETS.
- Any international consensus on post-bariatric hypoglycaemia diagnosis (search 3 returned 0; Salehi full text empty). Row 5 therefore rests on a review abstract, the PES statement and a CGM meta-analysis.
- Igls 2.0 as a published document; the numeric C-peptide threshold in Igls 2018 (full text unread).
- Classic reviews on NICTH (IGF-II ratio) and on insulin autoimmune syndrome before 2026; the IAS and NICTH items I could cite are 2026 reviews/case reports, read at abstract level.
- A guideline or review giving C-peptide cut-offs for type 3c diabetes, and an ADA or European guideline text on type 3c classification.
- Any guideline text on interpreting C-peptide in CKD for hypoglycaemia work-up.
- Evidence on diagnostic accuracy (sensitivity/specificity) of the insulin:C-peptide pattern for factitious insulin, and on insulin-analogue assay cross-reactivity by manufacturer.
- Registry searches (ClinicalTrials.gov): none run for this area.
