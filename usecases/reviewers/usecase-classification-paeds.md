Reviewer: ir-usecase-reviewer (area: DIABETES CLASSIFICATION AND PAEDIATRICS). Date of work: 2026-10-18.
Draft rows covered: 6, 7, 16, 19, and the paediatric aspects of 4. Coverage and reimbursement are out of scope.
Label key: SEC = secretion/classification use (C-peptide or insulin as a measure of beta-cell output). IR = insulin-resistance use. HYP = hypersecretion use. None of the rows in this area is a pure IR use except row 16 (paediatric obesity, where insulin is used as an IR/hyperinsulinaemia marker).

## Main-conclusion fit

The project conclusion (merged-conclusion.md) was not opened; the draft list's context line was used. Findings below agree with it: in this area insulin/C-peptide have narrow, defined classification roles, and insulin as an IR test in children is recommended against.
