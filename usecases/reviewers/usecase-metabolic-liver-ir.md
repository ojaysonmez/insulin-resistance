Reviewer: ir-usecase-reviewer role (teammate "metabolic-liver-ir"). Draft rows 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 17, 18, plus a search for missing uses. Coverage and reimbursement out of scope. Date of work: 2026-10-18.
Project files read: usecases-draft.md, merged-conclusion.md only.

How sources were read. "Full text (XML)" means the Europe PMC full-text XML was downloaded and searched with keyword windows, so I read the matching passages, not every page. "Full text (WebFetch)" means a small summarising model returned the passages I asked for, so quotes are the summariser's and are not verbatim-checked; I tag these and list what to re-check. "Abstract" and "title only" are as stated. [recall] = from memory, not retrieved.

Label for each use: IR = insulin-resistance use; SECRETION = C-peptide/secretion prognostic use; CLASS = classification/diagnostic use; RESEARCH = research endpoint or enrolment.

### Point for the main session
merged-conclusion calls the steelman's "top quartile" statement for HOMA-IR above 3.0 unsourced. The IRIS full text itself says the 3.0 threshold "identifies the highest quartile among populations without diabetes", so the claim has a primary source (the authors' own statement, no citation in the text I read). Row N3 above only repeats row 11 and can be ignored.

## 3. Common-knowledge, unsourced uses (not retrieved)

1. Fasting insulin or an OGTT insulin profile to support a diagnosis of "HAIR-AN" (hyperandrogenism, insulin resistance, acanthosis nigricans) in a woman with severe hyperandrogenism. [recall] The PCOS guideline lists severe IR syndromes as differentials, but I retrieved no criterion using an insulin value.
2. WHO 1999 metabolic syndrome definition requiring IR (clamp, HOMA or hyperinsulinaemia). [recall; historical]
3. Insulin and C-peptide in anti-insulin-receptor-antibody (type B) work-up are covered by the JDS table (sourced), not unsourced.
4. Very high fasting insulin or insulin requirements as a first clue to a rare IR syndrome (clinical practice). The dose-based clue is sourced from Table 3 of the lipodystrophy guideline; an assay-based clue outside JDS is [recall].
