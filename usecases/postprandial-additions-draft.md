## 3. Topics not turned into a row, or only citations

- T1 (mixed-meal test for reactive hypoglycaemia): D1 covers the non-surgical case. The postbariatric part adds citations only, for row 5: 40478434 (DOI 10.1007/s11695-025-07929-y, abstract: liquid MMTT did not separate postprandial reactive hypoglycaemic syndrome from non-PRHS after gastric bypass; solid-meal symptoms did, glucose responses were similar), 37445330 (DOI 10.3390/jcm12134295, abstract: MMTT confirmed post-bariatric hypoglycaemia (<54 mg/dL) in 88.9% of those >3 years from surgery versus 36.4% if shorter; CGM found more), 39406191 (DOI 10.1159/000541780, abstract: only 28% of 58 MMTs had a positive Sigstad score; glucose <70 mg/dL was not linked to symptoms). The main outcome in these was glucose and symptoms, not insulin. The rest of the T1 list is treatment trials, case reports or physiology and was not used.
- T2 (late dumping): no use for insulin or C-peptide found in the screened records. 34144916 (DOI 10.1016/j.soard.2021.05.020, abstract) found no insulin difference between dumping-symptom groups; GLP-1 and PYY differed. 37814558 (DOI 10.51821/86.3.11476, abstract) is a systematic review of prevalence and pathophysiology, with no test recommendation in the visible text. Citation for row 5 only.
- T3 (NIPHS): no new row. 37371827 (DOI 10.3390/biomedicines11061732, abstract) lists OGTT, 72-h fast, imaging and invasive insulin measurements as parts of the work-up but gives no test performance.
