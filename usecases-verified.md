# Verified use cases for insulin and C-peptide measurement (consolidated)

Purpose: one list of clinical, classification and research uses of insulin and C-peptide measurement, merging the 19-row draft (usecases-draft.md) with three reviewer reports (subagents/ir-usecase-hypo-pancreas.md, ir-usecase-classification-paeds.md, ir-usecase-metabolic-liver-ir.md). Date of consolidation: 2026-10-18 (reviewers' work also dated 2026-10-18).

Status of this document: statuses come from three reviewers' abstract-level or full-text-level work, then reviewed by a skeptic (ir-skeptic-usecases.md, Parts 1 and 2). Corrections from that review were applied on 2026-10-19 and are marked "skeptic" in the table. The skeptic did not re-verify the rows resting on abstracts or titles only (rows 5, 11, 19, 25, 27, 32, 35 cohort papers, 39-45) or the ISPAD, AAP 2023 and OPTN texts, so confidence there is lower. The consolidation itself added no claim that is not in the input files; merged-conclusion.md was read only for the comparison in section 6. Coverage and reimbursement are out of scope.

How to read it
- Reviewer tags: H = hypoglycaemia/pancreas/transplant reviewer, C = classification/paediatrics reviewer, M = metabolic/liver/IR reviewer. "H N3" means that reviewer's own item N3.
- Read levels: full text / abstract / title / [recall] (from memory, not retrieved). "Full text (summariser)" means the text came through a summarising fetch tool and quotes are not verbatim-checked. "Full text (keyword windows)" means searched passages were read, not every page.
- Status vocabulary was mapped by me to four values (established, limited, research-only, unsupported) from the reviewers' own wording, plus a flag, Not evaluated, for rows where the only sources bearing on the insulin or HOMA-IR question were read at title level or not retrieved. Not evaluated is not a finding of no utility. Where a reviewer's label did not fit cleanly, the original wording is in the caveats column.
- Kind: Secretion = secretion or hypersecretion use; Classification; IR = insulin-resistance use; Research. Reviewers sometimes gave a combined label (for example H "Secretion/classification", C "SEC" meaning secretion/classification, M "IR, CLASS"). I assigned one primary kind per row for counting, usually the first-named or the one matching the decision informed. Rows where this was a judgement are marked with an asterisk (*).
- Rows 1-19 are the draft rows. Rows 20-45 are new uses, renumbered from the reviewers' N-items. Reviewer M's N3 (repeats row 11) was dropped; M's N7 (kidney disease) was merged with H's N9 into row 28.
